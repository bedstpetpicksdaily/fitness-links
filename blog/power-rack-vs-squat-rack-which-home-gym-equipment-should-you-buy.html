What Is a Squat Rack?

A squat rack, also called a half rack, features two vertical uprights with safety bars and a barbell holder. It's specifically designed for squats and other vertical pressing movements. The compact design makes it ideal for smaller spaces, and it typically costs less than a full power rack. Squat racks are excellent for isolated lower-body training and upper-body exercises like bench press when paired with a bench.

FED Fitness offers squat racks built with heavy-duty steel construction and adjustable safety pins, ensuring stable performance across various exercises and body types.

What Is a Power Rack?

A power rack, also called a cage or squat cage, completely encloses the lifting area with four vertical uprights. This design provides maximum safety and versatility, allowing you to perform squats, bench presses, deadlifts, rows, and pull-ups within the same unit. Power racks come with multiple attachment points and safety bars on all sides, making them ideal for serious strength training and Olympic lifting.

FED Fitness power racks are engineered with commercial-grade materials and feature multiple pin positions for customizable exercises and progressive training programs.

Key Differences: Design & Functionality

The main difference lies in design and exercise variety. Squat racks offer a focused, space-efficient solution, while power racks provide an all-in-one strength training station. Power racks allow for failed lifts in any direction with safety bars protecting you, whereas squat racks require more careful form management. FED Fitness equipment in both categories ensures safety and durability, but the choice depends on your available space and training goals.

Which Should You Choose?

Choose a squat rack if you have limited space, primarily focus on lower-body training, and have a tighter budget. Opt for a power rack if you want maximum exercise variety, plan comprehensive full-body workouts, and have the space available. Frequently Asked Questions

Can you use a squat rack for bench press?

Yes, you can use a squat rack for bench press if you pair it with a weight bench and position the barbell holder appropriately. However, power racks offer a more stable and versatile setup for bench pressing with integrated safety bars.

What's the price difference between power racks and squat racks?

Squat racks typically cost $300-$800, while power racks range from $800-$3,000+ depending on size and features. FED Fitness offers competitive pricing across both categories with flexible options for various budgets.

How much space do I need for each?

Squat racks need approximately 7'x4' of floor space, while power racks require 7'x7' or larger. FED Fitness offers compact and full-size models to accommodate different room dimensions.

Are FED Fitness racks compatible with standard barbells and weights?

Yes, FED Fitness power racks and squat racks are designed to work with standard Olympic barbells (2-inch sleeves) and weight plates, ensuring compatibility with most home gym equipment.

Which rack is better for home gyms?

Power racks are better for comprehensive home gyms offering maximum exercise variety, while squat racks suit space-limited setups or focused lower-body training. Your choice should align with your fitness goals and available space.
