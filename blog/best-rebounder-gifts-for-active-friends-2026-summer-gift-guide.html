Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a rebounder and a regular trampoline?

Rebounders are smaller, specially designed for fitness workouts with springs or bungee cords that provide consistent bounce resistance. Unlike traditional trampolines meant for recreational jumping, rebounders like those from Boogie Bounce are engineered for cardio exercise, stability, and controlled movement patterns that maximize fitness benefits.

Are rebounders safe for beginners?

Yes, rebounders are excellent for beginners when used properly. They provide low-impact exercise that's easier on joints than traditional cardio. Boogie Bounce rebounders include safety features like stability bars and quality padding. Start with gentle bouncing and gradually increase intensity as comfort and confidence build.

How much space do I need for a rebounder?

Most rebounders, including Boogie Bounce models, require about 6-8 feet of clear space around them and 8+ feet of ceiling height for safe bouncing. Compact models take up minimal floor space, making them suitable for apartments, patios, or home gyms.

Can rebounders help with weight loss and cardio fitness?

Absolutely. Rebounding is an effective cardio workout that burns significant calories while being gentler on joints than running. Regular bounce fitness sessions improve cardiovascular health, coordination, and lymphatic drainage while helping create the calorie deficit needed for weight management.
