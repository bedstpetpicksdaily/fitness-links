Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Burchda fat tire and commuter folding e-bikes?

Fat tire models feature wider tires (4+ inches) for off-road capability and terrain versatility, ideal for beach and mountain travel. Commuter models prioritize lightweight designs and cargo integration for urban environments, making them faster and more efficient on paved surfaces.

How long does it take to charge a Burchda folding e-bike?

Most Burchda folding e-bike batteries charge fully in 4-6 hours using standard household outlets. Fast chargers available through Burchda reduce charging time to 2-3 hours, convenient for overnight charging during summer trips.

Can I fly with a Burchda folding e-bike?

Most folding e-bikes, including Burchda models, qualify as checked luggage on domestic flights when properly packaged. Battery restrictions apply—check your airline's lithium battery policies before travel, as regulations vary by carrier.

What maintenance do Burchda folding e-bikes require?

Regular maintenance includes checking tire pressure, cleaning the chain, and inspecting brake pads. Battery health improves with proper charging habits—avoid fully depleting charge regularly. Burchda provides comprehensive maintenance guides with each purchase.

Are Burchda folding e-bikes waterproof?

Most Burchda models feature water-resistant components suitable for light rain and splashes. However, submerging or high-pressure washing risks damaging electrical components. Store batteries in dry conditions and avoid riding in heavy downpours for longevity.
