Frequently Asked Questions

How do MiraFit adjustable dumbbells compare to PowerBlocks?

Both use plate-loading systems, but PowerBlocks charge roughly 40% more for faster plate access and compact stacking. MiraFit's mechanical reliability matches PowerBlocks' reputation, making it the smarter choice if budget matters. You're trading convenience for cost savings, not quality.

Will 52.5 lbs be heavy enough for upper body pressing?

For most home lifters, yes. That weight handles heavy dumbbell bench press, shoulder press, and rows. If you're already pressing 60+ lb dumbbells regularly, you've outgrown the top end. Check your current strength levels before assuming you need heavier.

Is the included stand necessary or just upselling?

The stand genuinely improves functionality. Loose adjustable dumbbells create clutter and make plate swaps awkward. The included stand is basic but adequate—you're not paying premium markup for unnecessary accessories here.

How does plate-loading compare to dial-adjustment dumbbells?

Plate-loading is slower between sets but mechanically simpler and more durable long-term. Dial systems offer faster transitions but add moving parts that eventually wear. Choose plate-loading if you value reliability; choose dial-based if speed matters more than longevity.

What's the actual footprint of the stand?

The stand is roughly 24 inches long and 12 inches wide—similar to a small weight bench footprint. Measure your space carefully if you're working with limited square footage. This isn't a compact option for tiny apartments.
