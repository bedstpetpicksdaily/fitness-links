How long does it take to charge a Kingbull e-bike?

Most Kingbull batteries charge fully in 4-6 hours using a standard wall outlet. Fast chargers are available for some models, reducing charge time to 2-3 hours. Partial charges are also supported if you need a quick top-up.

What's the weight difference between e-bike types?

Commuter e-bikes typically weigh 45-55 lbs, mountain e-bikes 55-65 lbs, fat tire models 65-75 lbs, and folding e-bikes 40-50 lbs. Weight varies based on motor size, battery capacity, and frame material.

Do I need a license or registration for an e-bike?

E-bike regulations vary by location. Most US states classify e-bikes as bicycles if they're under 750W and 28 mph. Check your local laws, as some states and cities have specific requirements regarding e-bike use on roads and trails.
