Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really need the iFit subscription, or can I just use the treadmill solo?

You can absolutely use it standalone—the touchscreen runs basic workout programs without subscription. But the interactive classes are where the real value lives. Live instructors adjusting your incline remotely during a hill workout, or personalized coaching based on your fitness level, transforms this from a machine you tolerate into one you anticipate. Most people find the subscription cost justifiable after their first month of actual consistency.

How loud is this thing? Will I wake my family at 5 AM?

The Commercial 1750 is noticeably quieter than budget treadmills, but it's not silent. At moderate speeds (6-8 mph), it's roughly 75-80 decibels—similar to a dishwasher running. Fast sprints will be louder. Placing it on a rubber mat or the included floor protector helps absorb vibration significantly. Early morning workouts are possible, but if your bedroom is directly above, headphones for the TV audio are essential to avoid waking sleepers.

Is the belt maintenance expensive or complicated?

NordicTrack includes belt lubrication packets, and the process takes maybe five minutes quarterly. No special tools required. The commercial-grade belt typically lasts 3-5 years with normal use before replacement becomes necessary. Replacement belts run $200-$300, which is reasonable for a high-use cardio machine. Regular maintenance prevents most problems; neglecting it leads to premature wear, so treat it like your car and you'll avoid expensive surprises.
