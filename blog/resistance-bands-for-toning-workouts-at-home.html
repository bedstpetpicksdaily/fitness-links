Why This Works for This Situation

Resistance bands are specifically designed for the kind of controlled, repetitive movements that build muscle definition and tone. Unlike dumbbells, which have fixed weights, bands provide variable resistance that increases as you stretch them further. This means the hardest part of your movement—when your muscles are most contracted—gets the most resistance, which is exactly what triggers the muscle activation needed for visible toning. You can perform high-repetition sets (15-25 reps) that are ideal for toning without the joint stress of heavy weights.

For home workouts specifically, resistance bands solve the storage problem that plagues many home gym enthusiasts.
