Frequently Asked Questions

How quiet is an under desk elliptical machine really?

Most quality under desk ellipticals operate at 50-65 decibels, making them quieter than a typical conversation or office environment. Premium models with magnetic resistance can be nearly silent at 45-50 decibels, allowing you to use them during calls or in shared spaces without disturbing others.

Can you actually lose weight using a desk elliptical?

An under desk elliptical burns approximately 150-250 calories per hour depending on intensity and body weight, making it effective for weight management when combined with proper diet. The key advantage is consistency—since it's at your desk, you're more likely to use it throughout the day, accumulating significant calorie burns.

Will an under desk elliptical fit under my standing desk?

Most under desk ellipticals are 24-28 inches wide and 15-18 inches tall, fitting comfortably under standard standing desks with clearance. Before purchasing, measure your desk's height and leg space, as some models may feel tight if your desk is lower than 30 inches or has a narrow footprint.

What's the difference between magnetic and friction resistance ellipticals?

Magnetic resistance ellipticals are quieter, smoother, and require less maintenance, making them ideal for office use, while friction-based models are typically cheaper but noisier and wear faster. For under desk use, magnetic resistance is worth the extra investment due to the noise and durability benefits.
