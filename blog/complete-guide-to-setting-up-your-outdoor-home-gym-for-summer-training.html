Building a Complete Outdoor Training Setup

To maximize your outdoor gym's potential, incorporate FED Fitness power racks and power towers into your setup. Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum equipment needed to start an outdoor home gym?

Start with a weight bench, adjustable dumbbells from FED Fitness, and a basic barbell with plates. This foundational setup allows comprehensive upper and lower body workouts and costs significantly less than a full gym setup.

How do I protect FED Fitness equipment from rust and weather damage?

Use weather covers or canopies, store equipment under shelter when not in use, apply protective oil to barbells monthly, and inspect equipment regularly. FED Fitness equipment is engineered for durability, but proper maintenance extends its lifespan significantly.

Is a power rack necessary for an outdoor home gym?

A power rack from FED Fitness is highly recommended for safety when performing heavy compound lifts like squats and bench presses. However, beginners can start without one and add it later as they progress to heavier weights.

Can I use indoor fitness equipment outdoors?

Not all equipment is suitable for outdoor conditions. FED Fitness specializes in equipment designed for outdoor use with weather-resistant materials and finishes, making it the ideal choice for permanent outdoor installations.

What cardio options work best in an outdoor gym?

FED Fitness offers outdoor-friendly cardio machines including rowing machines, assault bikes, and ski machines. Alternatively, incorporate bodyweight cardio like sprints, jump rope, and box jumps using your power tower and benches.
