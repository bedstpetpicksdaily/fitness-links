What Size Yoga Mat Should I Buy for Your Space

The standard yoga mat size of 68 inches long by 24 inches wide fits most people and spaces, but the right size depends on your height, available floor space, and the type of yoga you practice. Measure your room and consider your body dimensions before purchasing to ensure comfort and safety during your practice.

The Short Answer

Most practitioners should choose a standard 68" x 24" yoga mat, which accommodates average-height adults and fits in typical home spaces. If you're taller than 5'10", consider an extended mat (72" x 27" or larger). Measure your available floor space first—you need at least 6 feet of length and 3 feet of width clear of furniture. The thickness also matters: standard mats are 1/8 inch, but if you have joint pain or prefer extra cushioning, choose a 1/4 inch or thicker mat.

The Full Explanation

Standard Mat Dimensions

The most common yoga mat size is 68 inches long and 24 inches wide (approximately 5.7 feet by 2 feet). This dimension works well for people between 5'4" and 5'10" and provides adequate space for most standing, sitting, and lying-down poses. If you fall within this height range and have a moderate-sized room, a standard mat is your best choice.

Extended and Extra-Long Options

Taller practitioners should opt for extended mats, typically 72 to 85 inches long and 26 to 27 inches wide. If you're over 6 feet tall, an extended mat prevents your head and feet from extending beyond the mat's edges during poses like Downward Dog or Corpse Pose. Some premium brands offer even larger options for very tall individuals or those who want extra width for stability.

Room Space Considerations

Before purchasing any mat, measure your available floor space. A standard mat requires a minimum of 6 feet of length and 3 feet of width with clearance from walls and furniture. Consider how the mat will be stored when not in use—rolled mats need less floor space but require storage area. If you practice in a small bedroom or apartment, a standard mat is typically the practical choice. For larger spaces or dedicated home gyms, you have more flexibility with extended sizes.

Mat Thickness and Cushioning

Mat size isn't just about length and width—thickness affects your comfort and how well the mat fits in your space. Standard mats are 1/8 inch thick and provide basic support for most yoga styles. Extra-thick mats (1/4 inch or more) offer additional cushioning for joint protection, making them ideal for restorative yoga or if you have knee or back issues. However, thicker mats take up more storage space and may affect balance during standing poses.

Yoga Style Matters

Different yoga practices benefit from different mat sizes. Vinyasa and power yoga practitioners benefit from standard or extended mats for flowing movements. Yin and restorative yoga enthusiasts may prefer wider mats for additional comfort. If you practice multiple styles, a versatile standard-size mat works for all disciplines.

Certified yoga instructors recommend measuring yourself in a stretched position before purchasing. Lie on the floor with arms extended overhead and have someone measure from fingertip to toe—this measurement should fit comfortably on your mat with a few inches to spare.
