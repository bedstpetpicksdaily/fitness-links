Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between fat tire e-bikes and regular electric bikes?

Fat tire electric bikes feature oversized tires (typically 4 inches wide) that provide superior traction and comfort on loose, uneven surfaces. The wider tires absorb impact better, making fat tire e-bikes ideal for off-road trails, beaches, and snow. Regular e-bikes have narrower tires optimized for paved roads and urban commuting. Burchda offers both styles to suit different riding preferences.

How long does a Burchda fat tire e-bike battery last?

Burchda fat tire electric bikes typically provide 40-80 miles of range per full charge, depending on the model, terrain, and riding mode.
