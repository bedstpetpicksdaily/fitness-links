Frequently Asked Questions

Are Mooncool electric tricycles safe for seniors with balance issues?

Yes. The three-wheel design provides excellent stability and balance, making them much safer than two-wheeled bikes for seniors with balance concerns. The low center of gravity and wide wheelbase virtually eliminate tipping risk.

How far can I ride on a single charge with a Mooncool e-trike?

Most Mooncool models offer 30-50 miles of range per charge, depending on terrain, rider weight, and assist level. This covers typical daily activities like grocery shopping, visiting neighbors, and recreational park rides.

Do I have to pedal, or can I ride using only the electric motor?

Mooncool e-trikes offer both options. You can use full electric mode for days when you don't have energy to pedal, or use pedal-assist mode for light exercise. This flexibility lets you match your mobility solution to how you feel each day.

What's the weight limit for Mooncool electric tricycles?

Weight limits vary by model but typically range from 250-330 pounds. Check the specific model specifications when choosing your e-trike to ensure it meets your needs.

Can I use a Mooncool e-trike in the rain or wet conditions?

Mooncool e-trikes are built with weather-resistant components, making light rain riding safe. However, avoid riding in heavy downpours or flooding. Always store your e-trike indoors to protect the battery and electrical components.
