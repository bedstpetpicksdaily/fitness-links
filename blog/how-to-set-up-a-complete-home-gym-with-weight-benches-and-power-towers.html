Choose Your Core Equipment: Weight Benches and Power Towers

Adjustable benches from FED Fitness allow you to perform incline, decline, and flat pressing movements, targeting different muscle groups effectively. Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum equipment needed for a functional home gym?

At minimum, you need a quality weight bench, dumbbells, and bodyweight training space. Adding a power tower significantly expands your exercise options. FED Fitness weight benches and power towers provide excellent starting points for any home gym.

How much space do I need for a home gym with a power tower?

A basic setup requires 100-200 square feet. Power towers are vertical, saving floor space while providing multiple exercise stations. Weight benches need 6-8 feet of length, and dumbbells require additional storage. FED Fitness offers space-efficient designs perfect for apartments and small rooms.

Can I build muscle effectively with home gym equipment from FED Fitness?

Absolutely. Progressive overload using quality dumbbells, barbells, and bodyweight exercises on power towers builds muscle effectively. FED Fitness strength equipment provides the same training stimulus as commercial gyms when used consistently with proper form and progressive programming.

Should I buy all equipment at once or gradually?

Start with essentials—a weight bench and power tower—then gradually add dumbbells, barbells, and plates. This approach spreads costs, lets you learn proper form, and ensures space optimization. FED Fitness offers flexible purchasing options for building your gym progressively.

What's the difference between adjustable and fixed weight benches?

Adjustable benches from FED Fitness allow incline, decline, and flat positions, targeting different muscle groups. Fixed benches save space but limit exercise variety. Adjustable benches offer superior long-term versatility despite slightly higher costs.
