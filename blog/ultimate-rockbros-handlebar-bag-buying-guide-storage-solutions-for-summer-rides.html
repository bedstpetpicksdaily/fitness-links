Frequently Asked Questions

What's the weight capacity of Rockbros handlebar bags?

Most Rockbros handlebar bags safely carry 1.5-3 kg of gear, depending on the model size. Always check specific product specifications to ensure your bag can accommodate your intended load without affecting bike handling or stability.

Are Rockbros handlebar bags waterproof?

Many Rockbros handlebar bags feature water-resistant or weatherproof materials designed to protect contents from light rain and splashes. For extended heavy rain exposure, using waterproof packing cubes inside the bag adds extra protection for sensitive electronics like phones.

Can I use a Rockbros handlebar bag on drop bars or aerobars?

While Rockbros bags are primarily designed for flat and riser handlebars, some models feature adjustable mounting systems that work with drop bars. Check product specifications or contact Rockbros customer service to confirm compatibility with your specific bar type before purchasing.

How do I clean and maintain my Rockbros handlebar bag?

Hand wash your bag with mild soap and water, then air dry completely. Avoid submerging the bag entirely, as this can damage internal seals. Store in a cool, dry location when not in use, and periodically check mounting straps for wear or damage.
