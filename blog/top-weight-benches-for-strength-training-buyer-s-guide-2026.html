Frequently Asked Questions

What weight capacity do I need in a weight bench?

Choose a bench with a weight capacity at least 150-200 pounds above your projected maximum lift weight. For example, if you bench press 300 lbs, opt for a bench rated for 450+ lbs to ensure safety and longevity. FED Fitness benches offer capacities up to 1000+ pounds for serious strength athletes.

Should I buy an adjustable or flat weight bench?

Adjustable benches offer more exercise variety (incline, decline, flat positions) and cost more, while flat benches provide simplicity and maximum stability for heavy compound movements. Beginners benefit from adjustable benches, while experienced lifters often prefer flat benches for pure strength work. FED Fitness offers both options to match your training style.

Can I use a weight bench for exercises besides bench press?

What's the difference between commercial and home gym benches?

Commercial benches feature heavier steel frames, premium padding, and higher weight capacities to withstand constant use. Home gym benches prioritize space efficiency while maintaining durability.
