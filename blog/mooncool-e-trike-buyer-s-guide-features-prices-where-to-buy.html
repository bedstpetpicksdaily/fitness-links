Most Mooncool models offer 40-60 miles of range per charge, depending on the specific model, terrain, riding style, and weather conditions. Using pedal-assist mode can extend your range further.

Mooncool typically offers manufacturer warranty coverage on their e-trikes, including coverage for the frame, motor, and battery. Specific warranty terms vary by model, so check the product details or contact their customer support for comprehensive information.
