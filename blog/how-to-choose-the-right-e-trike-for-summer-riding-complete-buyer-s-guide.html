Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between an e-trike and an electric bike?

E-trikes have three wheels for superior stability and typically feature more robust frames for comfortable seating positions. Electric bikes (e-bikes) have two wheels and are lighter but require more balance. E-trikes are ideal for seniors and those seeking maximum stability during summer riding.

How long do e-trike batteries last?

Quality lithium batteries, like those in Mooncool e-trikes, typically last 3-5 years with proper care. Most maintain 80% capacity after 500-1000 charge cycles. Proper storage and regular charging extend battery lifespan significantly.

Are e-trikes suitable for seniors?

Yes, e-trikes are excellent for seniors. They provide stable seating, motorized assistance that reduces physical strain, and comfortable riding positions. Mooncool specifically designs models for seniors with ergonomic features and safety enhancements.

What maintenance do e-trikes require?

E-trikes need regular tire pressure checks, brake inspections, chain lubrication, and battery maintenance. Unlike gas vehicles, they require minimal maintenance. Mooncool provides detailed maintenance guides with each purchase.

Can I ride an e-trike in summer heat?

Yes, e-trikes perform well in summer heat. Ensure proper battery ventilation and avoid extreme temperatures. Mooncool batteries include thermal management systems designed for various weather conditions.
