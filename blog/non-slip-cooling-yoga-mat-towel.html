What to Look For

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a non-slip cooling yoga mat towel actually stay in place during hot yoga?

Yes, non-slip cooling yoga mat towels use specialized rubber or silicone grip technology on the underside to prevent sliding during sweaty practices like hot yoga and power yoga. The grip becomes more effective as you sweat, providing better traction than standard towels that typically bunch up or shift during intense sessions.

How long does the cooling effect last on a yoga mat towel?

Most cooling yoga mat towels maintain their temperature-reducing properties for 30-60 minutes of active use, depending on the material and room temperature. The cooling is typically achieved through moisture-wicking microfiber technology that allows sweat to evaporate quickly, rather than phase-change materials that have a limited duration.

Can you machine wash a non-slip cooling yoga mat towel?

Most non-slip cooling yoga mat towels are machine washable in cold water on a gentle cycle, but you should avoid fabric softener and high heat drying as these can damage the non-slip grip coating. Check the manufacturer's care instructions, as some premium towels recommend air drying to extend the lifespan of the non-slip base.

What size non-slip cooling yoga mat towel do I need?

Standard yoga mat towels are typically 24" x 72" to fit a full-size yoga mat, while some brands offer longer 26" x 82" options for extra coverage on the sides and ends. Measure your mat before purchasing to ensure adequate coverage, as undersized towels may shift during poses.
