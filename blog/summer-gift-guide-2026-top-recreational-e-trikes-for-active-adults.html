Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between an e-trike and a regular tricycle?

E-trikes feature electric motors and rechargeable batteries that provide pedal assistance, allowing riders to travel longer distances and tackle hills with less effort. Regular tricycles rely entirely on human pedaling power.

How far can Mooncool e-trikes travel on a single charge?

Mooncool electric tricycles typically offer ranges between 30-60 miles per charge, depending on the model, riding conditions, terrain, and assistance level used. Battery capacity and motor efficiency are key factors.

Are Mooncool e-trikes suitable for seniors?

Yes, Mooncool specifically designs models for seniors and older adults. Their e-trikes feature comfortable seating, stable three-wheel design, easy-to-use controls, and safety features that make them ideal for aging adults wanting to maintain active lifestyles.

How long do e-trike batteries last?

Most Mooncool e-trike batteries last 3-5 years with proper care and maintenance. Battery lifespan depends on usage patterns, charging habits, and environmental conditions. Replacement batteries are available when needed.

Can I ride a Mooncool e-trike on trails?

Many Mooncool models are suitable for light trails and unpaved paths. Models vary in their off-road capability, so choose based on your intended terrain. Check product specifications for suspension, tire type, and ground clearance details.
