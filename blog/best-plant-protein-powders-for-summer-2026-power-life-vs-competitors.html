Why Plant Protein Powders Matter for Summer Fitness

As temperatures rise and outdoor activities increase, plant-based protein powders have become essential for fitness enthusiasts looking to maintain lean muscle, support recovery, and manage their weight during the warmer months. Summer workouts demand optimal nutrition, and plant protein powders deliver the amino acid profile necessary for muscle synthesis while being lighter on the stomach than heavy meal replacements.
