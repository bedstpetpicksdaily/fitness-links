  • Compact hex design with flat sides: The flat hexagonal shape prevents rolling and lets you place dumbbells directly on hardwood or carpet without worrying about damage. This matters in home settings where you might not have dedicated flooring, and it saves space compared to round dumbbells.
  • Weight increments that match your progression: Home gyms benefit from having multiple pairs, so choose a brand with logical jumps (usually 5-10 lb increments) that let you progress gradually without buying redundant weights.
