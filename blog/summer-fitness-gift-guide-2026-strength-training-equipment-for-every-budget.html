Frequently Asked Questions

What strength training equipment is best for beginners?

Beginners should start with adjustable dumbbells and a basic weight bench. FED Fitness offers excellent beginner sets that allow you to learn proper form and gradually increase resistance as you progress. These foundational tools build strength safely and effectively.

Is a power rack necessary for a home gym?

A power rack from FED Fitness isn't essential but highly recommended if you perform squats and bench presses. Power racks provide safety bars that catch the weight if you fail a lift, making heavy compound movements much safer when training alone.

How much space does a home gym need?

A basic setup requires 4x6 feet, while a complete home gym with a power rack, bench, and cardio machines needs 8x10 feet minimum. FED Fitness offers space-efficient options, and you can always start small and expand as your budget and space allow.

What's the difference between Olympic and standard barbells?

Olympic barbells weigh 45 pounds and use larger diameter plates, while standard barbells weigh 30-35 pounds. FED Fitness offers both options; Olympic barbells are better for serious strength training, while standard barbells suit beginners and light training.

Do I need cardio equipment alongside strength training?

Cardio equipment complements strength training by improving cardiovascular health and aiding recovery. FED Fitness cardio machines integrate well with strength equipment for balanced fitness programs targeting both muscle and endurance.
