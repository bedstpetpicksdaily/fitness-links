Understanding Fabric Technology in Moisture-Wicking Apparel

The foundation of great fitness apparel lies in its fabric composition. Look for materials specifically engineered to manage moisture, such as polyester blends, nylon, or innovative synthetics that pull sweat away from your skin faster than cotton. These technical fabrics work by capillary action, moving moisture to the outer surface where it can evaporate efficiently. Cotton absorbs moisture and holds it against your skin, leading to discomfort and potential chafing.
