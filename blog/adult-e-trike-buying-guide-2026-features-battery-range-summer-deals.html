Frequently Asked Questions

What battery range should I realistically expect from a summer 2026 e-trike deal?

Promotional materials claim extended range without publishing specific mileage. Real-world range depends on rider weight, terrain, assistance level, and weather—typically 20-30% lower than marketing estimates. Request concrete range specifications (e.g., '45 miles at 50% assistance on flat terrain') before purchase rather than relying on vague 'extended battery' claims.

Are Mooncool e-trikes suitable for riders over 300 lbs?

Weight capacity specifications appear in FAQ sections but rarely correlate published limits with motor power or battery range reduction. Confirm exact weight capacity and request range estimates for your body weight, as heavier riders experience proportionally reduced mileage. Some models may reduce usable range by 25-40% for higher-weight users.

Do summer 2026 warranty extensions actually cover battery degradation?

Extended battery warranties bundled in summer deals typically cover manufacturing defects, not normal degradation. Batteries in e-trikes degrade 2-3% annually; warranties usually cover catastrophic failure but not incremental range loss. Review specific warranty terms before assuming summer bundled deals include replacement coverage.

How does the pedal-assist system actually work—can I ride without pedaling?

True pedal-assist requires foot input to trigger motor assistance (sensor-based or torque-based). Full electric throttle-only mode, if available, may be limited or absent on many recreational e-trikes prioritizing exercise engagement. Clarify whether your intended use case matches the specific assistance model (pedal-required vs. throttle-capable).

What's the actual monthly maintenance cost for a Mooncool e-trike?

Summer promotions mention simplified controls and low maintenance compared to bikes, but provide no maintenance cost breakdown. Typical monthly costs include tire pressure checks, brake inspection, and occasional lubrication—probably $0 in DIY costs, or $30-60 annually if outsourced to a service center. Budget separately for battery replacement (~$400-700) every 3-5 years depending on usage.
