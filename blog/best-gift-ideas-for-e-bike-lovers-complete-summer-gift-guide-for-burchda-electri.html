Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between fat tire and regular e-bikes?

Fat tire electric bikes feature oversized, low-pressure tires (4-5 inches wide) that provide superior traction, stability, and comfort on challenging terrain like sand, snow, and rocky trails. They're heavier and slower on pavement but excel in off-road conditions. Regular e-bikes are lighter and faster on smooth surfaces, making them better for road and commuting use.

Are Burchda folding electric bikes suitable for daily commuting?

Yes, absolutely. Burchda's commuter e-bikes and folding models are specifically designed for daily urban commuting. They offer powerful motors for easy pedaling, compact designs for storage, and reliable performance. The folding feature makes them ideal for multi-modal commutes combining e-bikes with public transportation.

What accessories should every e-bike owner have?

Essential e-bike accessories include safety lights, a quality helmet, a reliable lock, repair tools, spare tubes, a portable charger, and weatherproof bags. For Burchda e-bike owners, adding a maintenance kit and suspension upgrades can further enhance the riding experience and bike longevity.

How long do e-bike batteries last on a summer ride?

Most Burchda e-bikes offer 25-50 miles per charge depending on terrain, rider weight, assist level, and temperature. Summer heat can slightly improve battery performance compared to winter conditions, making summer ideal for longer adventures with your electric bike.

Are electric bikes good for beginners?

Yes, e-bikes are excellent for beginners. The electric assistance makes riding easier, reduces fatigue, and builds confidence. Burchda's range of commuter e-bikes and user-friendly models are particularly well-suited for those new to electric biking, allowing them to enjoy longer rides comfortably.
