Yes, electric tricycles are excellent for seniors. The three-wheel design provides superior stability compared to bicycles, and the assisted pedaling reduces physical strain. Mooncool e-trikes include safety features like integrated lighting, reflectors, and ergonomic braking systems designed specifically for older riders.

How far can a Mooncool electric tricycle travel on one charge?

Most Mooncool e-trikes offer 40+ miles of range per charge, depending on terrain, rider weight, and assist level. This range makes them ideal for extended outdoor rides and recreational trips throughout your community.

What's the weight capacity of Mooncool electric tricycles?

Mooncool designs their e-trikes to support riders up to 350+ lbs, with reinforced frames and components built for durability and safety. Always check specific model specifications for exact weight capacity.
