Frequently Asked Questions

What weight capacity do I need for an adult mini trampoline?

Most quality adult mini trampolines support 250-300 lbs, with premium models reaching 400+ lbs. Check the manufacturer's specifications carefully, as exceeding the weight limit reduces bounce quality and can damage the springs or frame. If you're near the upper range, opt for a model with a higher capacity for safety and longevity.

Is a mini trampoline good for cardio and weight loss?

Yes, rebounding on a mini trampoline can burn 200-300 calories per 30-minute session while being low-impact on your joints. It's particularly effective for cardiovascular fitness and engages your core, legs, and stabilizer muscles. For weight loss, combine regular rebounding with a proper diet and strength training.

What's the difference between a mini trampoline with and without a handlebar?

A handlebar provides stability and balance support, making it ideal for beginners, older adults, or those recovering from injuries. Trampolines without handbars offer more freedom of movement and better for advanced exercises like high-intensity interval training. Choose based on your fitness level and workout style.

How much space does a mini trampoline take up and can it be stored?

Most adult mini trampolines measure 40-50 inches in diameter and require about 100-150 square feet of clear space around them for safe use. Many models have foldable designs that collapse to 12-18 inches tall, making them easy to store in closets or under beds when not in use.
