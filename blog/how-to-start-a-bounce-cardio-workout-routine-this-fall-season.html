Frequently Asked Questions

Is bounce cardio safe for beginners?

Yes, bounce cardio is extremely beginner-friendly and safer than many traditional cardio methods. The rebound surface of quality equipment like Boogie Bounce rebounders absorbs impact, protecting your joints while you exercise. Start with low-intensity movements and progress gradually as your balance and confidence improve.

How often should I do bounce cardio workouts?

As a beginner, aim for 3-4 bounce cardio sessions per week with rest days between sessions. Each session can last 15-30 minutes as you build endurance. This frequency allows your body to adapt while preventing overuse injuries.

What's the difference between a rebounder and a mini trampoline?

While often used interchangeably, rebounders typically refer to larger, more stable bounce fitness equipment designed specifically for workouts, while mini trampolines are smaller and more portable. Boogie Bounce offers both types to suit different needs and spaces.

Can I lose weight with bounce cardio?

Absolutely. Bounce cardio burns significant calories while improving cardiovascular health, making it effective for weight loss when combined with proper nutrition. A consistent routine using quality Boogie Bounce equipment can deliver impressive results over time.

Do I need special shoes for bounce workouts?

While not required, supportive athletic shoes with good cushioning are recommended for bounce cardio. They provide additional joint protection and stability, especially during longer workout sessions.
