Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the Ninja Play Fitness Armband's heart rate monitoring?

The Ninja Play Fitness Armband uses advanced optical sensors that provide consistently accurate heart rate readings, even during high-intensity interval training. Most users report accuracy within 1-2 beats per minute compared to medical-grade monitors.

What does 'adaptive training zones' mean?

Adaptive training zones automatically adjust your heart rate training ranges based on your fitness level, recovery status, and workout history. Rather than using static zones, the Ninja Play Fitness Armband continuously learns your cardiovascular patterns to provide personalized training guidance.

Is the Ninja Play Fitness Armband waterproof for swimming?

The armband is water-resistant and can handle sweat, rain, and splashes. However, it's not fully waterproof for swimming. It's best suited for functional training, gym workouts, and outdoor activities rather than pool workouts.

How long does the battery last?

With typical daily use, the Ninja Play Fitness Armband provides approximately 7-10 days of battery life between charges, depending on usage intensity and heart rate monitoring frequency.

Can I use the Ninja Play Fitness Armband with any smartphone?

The companion app is compatible with both iOS and Android devices. You'll need a smartphone to access full features and detailed analytics, though basic heart rate data is viewable on the device itself.
