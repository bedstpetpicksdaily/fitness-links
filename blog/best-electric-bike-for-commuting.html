What We Looked For

Frequently Asked Questions

How many miles can an electric bike go on a single charge?

Most commuter e-bikes offer 25-50 miles per charge, depending on motor power (250W-750W), battery capacity (400-700Wh), and terrain. If your commute is under 20 miles round-trip, a mid-range e-bike will easily get you there and back without recharging. For longer distances, look for models with 600Wh+ batteries or dual battery options.

Will an electric bike help me stay fit or does it replace exercise?

E-bikes keep you active through pedaling while providing motor assistance when needed—you're still engaging your cardiovascular system and leg muscles, just with less strain. Many fitness enthusiasts use commute e-bikes on easy mode for light activity or switch off assist on weekends for a harder workout, making them perfect for maintaining fitness while reducing commute fatigue.

What's the difference between pedal-assist and throttle e-bikes for commuting?

Pedal-assist (PAS) requires you to pedal and provides motor support proportional to your effort, offering better range and a more natural cycling feel. Throttle-only e-bikes let you ride without pedaling but drain battery faster; many commuters prefer PAS models since they combine exercise benefits with efficiency and are legal in most areas.

How much weight can an electric commuter bike support?

Most quality commuter e-bikes support 250-300 lbs of total weight (rider plus cargo), though premium models go up to 350+ lbs. If you're heavier or plan to carry significant cargo, check the manufacturer's specs and consider a model with a reinforced frame and higher-wattage motor (750W+) to maintain performance.
