Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I use a rebounder for fitness results?

For optimal results, aim for 15-30 minutes of rebounding, 3-5 times weekly. Beginners should start with shorter sessions and gradually increase duration as their body adapts to the low-impact cardio workout.

Is rebounding safe for people with joint problems?

Rebounding is an excellent low-impact exercise option for joint issues because it reduces stress on knees and hips compared to running. However, always consult your healthcare provider before starting any new fitness routine, especially with existing conditions.

What's the difference between bungee and spring rebounders?

Spring rebounders use metal coils and offer a bouncier, more responsive feel. Bungee rebounders use elastic cords for a softer, quieter bounce that's easier on joints. Boogie Bounce offers both options depending on your preference and fitness goals.

Do I need assembly help for a Boogie Bounce rebounder?

Most Boogie Bounce rebounders come partially assembled with clear instructions. Assembly typically takes 15-30 minutes and requires basic tools. Customer support is available if you need additional guidance.

Can rebounders help with weight loss?

Yes, rebounding burns significant calories while being enjoyable, making it easier to maintain consistency. Combined with proper nutrition, regular rebounding can effectively support weight loss and improve overall fitness.
