Top Cardio Machines for Summer Success

Treadmills remain the most popular cardio choice, offering versatility for runners of all levels. You can adjust speed and incline to simulate outdoor conditions or create interval training protocols. Stationary bikes provide low-impact alternatives perfect for those with joint concerns, delivering intense calorie burns without stress on knees and hips. Elliptical machines combine upper and lower body engagement while minimizing impact, making them ideal for long, steady-state sessions. Rowing machines deliver full-body cardio benefits, engaging your back, core, and legs while also building muscular endurance that complements strength training with FED Fitness equipment.

What's the best cardio machine for small spaces?

How often should I do cardio alongside strength training?

Most fitness experts recommend 2-4 cardio sessions weekly (20-40 minutes each) combined with 3-4 strength sessions. This balance, supported by FED Fitness equipment, builds both cardiovascular fitness and muscular strength without overtraining.
