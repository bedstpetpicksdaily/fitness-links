Why Hot Weather Workout Clothes Matter

Temperature regulation during exercise is crucial for athletic performance. When your body overheats, your heart works harder, you fatigue faster, and injury risk increases. Standard cotton clothing traps moisture against your skin, creating an uncomfortable, heavy feeling that restricts movement. APE BORN recognizes these challenges and designs apparel that actively works with your body's natural cooling mechanisms, not against them.
