Frequently Asked Questions

Are Mooncool e-trikes suitable for seniors?

Absolutely. Mooncool specifically designs their electric tricycles with seniors in mind. Features like ergonomic seats, pedal-assist modes that reduce physical strain, stable three-wheel designs, and easy mounting make them ideal for older riders. The electric motor assistance allows seniors to ride longer distances without exhaustion.

How far can a Mooncool e-trike travel on a single charge?

Most Mooncool models offer 30-50+ miles of range per charge, depending on the specific model, battery capacity, terrain, and riding mode used. Pedal-assist mode typically provides longer range than full electric mode, as it combines human power with motor assistance.

What warranty does Mooncool offer on their e-trikes?

Mooncool provides comprehensive warranties covering frames, motors, and batteries. Most models include 1-2 year manufacturer warranties, with extended protection options available. Check the specific model details and current promotions on their website for exact coverage information.

Can I ride a Mooncool e-trike in the rain?

Mooncool e-trikes are built with weather-resistant components, making them suitable for light rain and wet conditions. However, avoid heavy downpours and always dry the tricycle afterward. Most models have water-resistant electrical connections and sealed batteries for added protection.

How long does it take to charge a Mooncool e-trike battery?

Most Mooncool batteries fully charge in 4-6 hours using the standard charger. Some premium models include fast-charging options that reduce charging time to 2-3 hours. Always refer to your specific model's manual for accurate charging times and battery care recommendations.
