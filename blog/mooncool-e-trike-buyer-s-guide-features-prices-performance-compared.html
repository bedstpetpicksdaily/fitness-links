Battery Performance & Range Comparison

One of the most critical factors in choosing an e-trike is battery capacity and real-world range. Mooncool e-trikes typically come equipped with lithium-ion batteries ranging from 36V to 48V systems, with amp-hour ratings between 10Ah and 20Ah. These specifications translate to riding ranges of 30-60 miles per charge, depending on terrain, rider weight, and pedaling assistance level. Mooncool batteries are removable for convenient charging at home, office, or while traveling. Fast-charging capabilities mean you can achieve 80% battery capacity in 4-6 hours, making daily use practical for commuters and recreational enthusiasts alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the weight capacity of Mooncool e-trikes?

Most Mooncool e-trike models support riders up to 300-330 pounds, with reinforced frames and components rated for this capacity. Always verify specific weight ratings with your chosen model.

Are Mooncool e-trikes suitable for seniors?

Yes, absolutely. Mooncool specifically designs e-trikes for senior riders with features like easy step-through frames, comfortable padded seats, stable three-wheel design, and intuitive controls that make them ideal for older adults.

How long does a Mooncool e-trike battery last before replacement?

Lithium-ion batteries in Mooncool e-trikes typically last 500-1,000 charge cycles, translating to 2-4 years of regular use. Proper maintenance and storage extend battery lifespan significantly.

Can you ride a Mooncool e-trike without using electric assistance?

Yes, Mooncool e-trikes can be pedaled manually like traditional tricycles. Many riders switch between pedaling and electric assist depending on energy levels and terrain conditions.
