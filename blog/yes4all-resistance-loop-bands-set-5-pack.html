Pros

Will these bands actually build muscle or are they just for "toning"?

Yes, they can build muscle—if you use progressive overload and proper form. The issue is that Yes4All's 5-pack only takes you so far. Once you stop getting sore and the heaviest band feels easy, you're maxed out. That typically happens within 3-6 months for someone training consistently. Based on the 500+ reviews: expect 6-12 months if you're doing 3-4 workouts per week with proper form and care. Keep them away from heat and rough surfaces, and they'll outlast the people who trash them immediately.
