Frequently Asked Questions

Are Rockbros bike bags truly waterproof?

Yes, Rockbros bike bags feature waterproof materials and sealed seams specifically engineered to protect contents from rain, splashes, and moisture. However, extended submersion isn't recommended. For maximum protection, pair bags with waterproof dry bags for sensitive items.

What's the weight capacity of Rockbros panniers?

Most Rockbros panniers support 10-15kg per side, with heavier-duty models accommodating up to 20kg. Always verify specific product specifications and ensure weight distribution remains balanced across both sides for optimal bike handling.

Can Rockbros bags fit on my bike?

Rockbros bags feature universal mounting systems compatible with most road, mountain, and hybrid bikes. Handlebar bags fit standard bars, and pannier racks work with most rear wheel configurations. Check product specifications for your specific bike type before purchasing.

How do I clean and maintain Rockbros bike bags?

Wipe exteriors with a damp cloth and mild soap. Allow bags to air dry completely before storage. Avoid machine washing or harsh chemicals. Apply silicone spray to zippers occasionally to maintain smooth operation and water resistance.

Are Rockbros bags suitable for bikepacking?

Absolutely. Rockbros offers dedicated bikepacking solutions including multiple pannier options, frame bags, and handlebar bags that work together to create comprehensive cargo systems for extended summer adventures and multi-day tours.
