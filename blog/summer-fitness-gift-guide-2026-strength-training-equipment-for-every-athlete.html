Frequently Asked Questions

What strength equipment is best for a beginner?

For beginners, dumbbells and an adjustable weight bench from FED Fitness are excellent starting points. They're versatile, affordable compared to racks, and allow safe progression as strength improves. Dumbbells enable hundreds of exercises with minimal investment.

Do I need a power rack for home training?

A power rack significantly enhances home gym capabilities, especially for compound lifts like squats and bench presses. FED Fitness racks provide safety spotters and adjustable heights. However, dumbbells and a weight bench can suffice for those starting out or with space limitations.

How do I choose between fixed-weight and adjustable dumbbells?

Fixed-weight dumbbells are more durable and feel premium but require more space. Adjustable dumbbells save space and money upfront. FED Fitness offers both options—choose based on your space, budget, and whether you prefer quick adjustments during workouts.

Are FED Fitness barbells suitable for powerlifting?

Yes! FED Fitness offers competition-grade barbells that meet strict standards. Their precision engineering, reliable spin, and accurate weight make them suitable for serious powerlifting training and competitions.

What should I prioritize in a strength training gift?

Consider the recipient's goals, available space, and current experience level. Quality dumbbells work for anyone, while specialized equipment like racks suit dedicated athletes. FED Fitness offers options at every level to match your budget and needs.
