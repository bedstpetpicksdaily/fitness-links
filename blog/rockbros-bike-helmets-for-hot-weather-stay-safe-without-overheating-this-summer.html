How do ventilated helmets compare to solid helmets in terms of safety?

Both ventilated and solid helmets meet the same safety standards (CPSC in the US). Ventilated helmets prioritize airflow while maintaining impact protection. Rockbros helmets are engineered to pass all safety tests while maximizing breathability for summer use.
