Frequently Asked Questions

What's the actual adjustment mechanism on Mirafit dumbbells?

Mirafit adjustable sets typically use a pin-and-plate system where you select the weight by inserting a pin into numbered slots. This is simpler than dial mechanisms but requires you to locate the correct pin each time. It's reliable for casual use but slower than dial adjustments if you're supersetting heavy exercises.

Will these handle serious progressive overload, or are they just for maintenance?

A 25kg dumbbell per hand supports genuine strength training for most people. That's 50kg total for dumbbell bench press, rows, or squats. You won't be setting PRs at advanced levels, but beginners through intermediate lifters can definitely build muscle and strength. Beyond that, you'll outgrow the set.

How much floor space does the stand actually take up?

Most adjustable dumbbell stands are roughly 60cm wide and 30cm deep. If your home gym is a corner of your bedroom, verify your space before ordering. The stand prevents clutter but demands dedicated real estate—it's not designed to be tucked away.

Are there weight gaps that make progression awkward?

Standard adjustable sets jump in 2.5kg increments, which matches most programming. You won't find yourself jumping from 15kg to 20kg with no middle ground. This is a genuine strength of the format compared to fixed dumbbells where you might own 10kg, 12kg, and 15kg with gaps in between.

Should I trust a 4.3-star rating with 500+ reviews?

A 4.3 rating across 500 reviews is meaningful—it means the product survived enough real-world use to reveal patterns. That said, 500 reviews could hide a 10% failure rate that manifests after year two. Check the most recent reviews specifically; older reviews don't predict current manufacturing quality.
