Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical range of an e-trike on a single charge?

Premium e-trikes like those from Mooncool typically offer 30-60+ miles per charge, depending on motor power, battery capacity, riding conditions, and rider weight. Most recreational riders find this range sufficient for daily excursions and leisure rides.

Are e-trikes suitable for seniors with limited mobility?

Yes, e-trikes are specifically designed for seniors. The three-wheel design eliminates balance concerns, the electric motor reduces physical exertion demands, and features like comfortable seating and intuitive controls make Mooncool e-trikes particularly senior-friendly.

How long does it take to charge an e-trike battery?

Most premium e-trike batteries require 4-8 hours for a full charge, though quick-charge options may be available. Mooncool provides detailed specifications for their models to help you plan charging schedules effectively.

Can I use an e-trike in rain or wet conditions?

Most quality e-trikes, including Mooncool models, feature weather-resistant components. However, it's recommended to avoid prolonged exposure to heavy rain and to dry the trike after wet rides to maintain optimal performance.

Do I need a license or registration for an e-trike?

Regulations vary by location. Many areas don't require licenses for e-trikes under specific power limits (typically under 750W). Check your local regulations, and Mooncool can provide guidance based on your region.
