Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between adjustable and flat weight benches?

Flat benches are fixed and occupy minimal space, while adjustable benches offer multiple incline/decline positions for varied exercises. Adjustable benches provide more versatility but require additional room. FED Fitness offers both types to match different home gym needs.

How much weight capacity do I need in a home gym bench?

Choose a bench with capacity exceeding your current lifts by 100+ pounds. Most quality benches support 400-600 lbs. FED Fitness benches meet or exceed these standards, ensuring durability as your strength increases.

Can I use a weight bench with dumbbells, barbells, and cables?

Yes—adjustable benches work with dumbbells and barbells. Some FED Fitness models include leg extension attachments. Cables require separate pulley systems, though benches can position you for cable exercises performed from nearby stations.

How do I prevent a weight bench from sliding during workouts?

Quality benches feature non-slip feet and heavy bases. Place your bench on rubber mats for additional grip. FED Fitness benches include stabilizing feet designed to prevent movement during intense training.

What's the best weight bench for small spaces?

Opt for compact, flat benches or foldable adjustable models. FED Fitness offers space-conscious designs that fold for storage while maintaining structural integrity when deployed.
