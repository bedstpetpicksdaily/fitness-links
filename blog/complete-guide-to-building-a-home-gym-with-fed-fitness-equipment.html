Frequently Asked Questions

What FED Fitness equipment should I buy first for my home gym?

Start with adjustable dumbbells and a weight bench from FED Fitness. These versatile pieces allow you to perform dozens of exercises and provide an excellent foundation. As your fitness progresses and budget allows, add barbells, weight plates, and eventually a power rack.

How much space do I need for a home gym with FED Fitness equipment?

A basic setup with dumbbells and a bench requires approximately 100-200 square feet. If you plan to add a power rack or cardio machines from FED Fitness, aim for 200-400 square feet. Measure your space before purchasing to ensure proper fit and safe exercise execution.

Are FED Fitness dumbbells and barbells suitable for beginners?

Absolutely. FED Fitness offers equipment across all weight ranges and price points, making it ideal for beginners. Their adjustable dumbbells and lighter barbell options allow you to start safely and progress gradually as your strength increases.

Do I need both a power rack and power tower from FED Fitness?

Not necessarily. Choose based on your goals and space. Power racks accommodate heavy barbell lifts and provide maximum versatility. Power towers are space-efficient and excellent for bodyweight exercises. Select whichever aligns with your primary training focus.

How do I maintain FED Fitness cardio equipment?

Keep machines clean by wiping them down after use, check belts and moving parts regularly, lubricate as needed per manufacturer instructions, and store in a climate-controlled environment when possible. Proper maintenance ensures longevity and consistent performance.
