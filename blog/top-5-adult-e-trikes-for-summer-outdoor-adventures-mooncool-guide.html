What is the average battery range for adult e-trikes?

Most quality adult e-trikes, including Mooncool models, offer 30-50 miles of range per full charge, depending on terrain, rider weight, and motor assistance level. Lithium-ion batteries provide reliable performance for full-day summer adventures.

Are electric tricycles suitable for seniors?

Yes, e-trikes are excellent for seniors. The stable three-wheel design, electric motor assistance, and comfortable seating eliminate many challenges associated with traditional cycling. Mooncool specifically designs models with senior comfort and safety in mind.

How long does it take to charge an e-trike battery?

Most e-trike batteries charge fully in 4-6 hours using standard household outlets. Mooncool provides fast-charging options that can reduce charge times to 3-4 hours, perfect for planning consecutive-day summer adventures.
