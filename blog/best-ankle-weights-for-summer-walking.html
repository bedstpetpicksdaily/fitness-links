Frequently Asked Questions

Are ankle weights good for summer walking?

Yes, ankle weights can increase calorie burn and strengthen your legs during summer walks, but they add extra stress to your joints and can cause overheating. Start with lighter weights (1-3 lbs) and ensure proper form to avoid injury.

What weight should ankle weights be for walking?

For walking, use 1-3 pound ankle weights if you're a beginner, and 3-5 pounds if you're experienced with strength training. Heavier weights aren't necessary for walking and can strain your knees and ankles.

Which ankle weights are best for hot weather?

Look for lightweight, breathable ankle weights with moisture-wicking material and neoprene construction to minimize heat retention. Brands like Tone Fitness and CAP Barbell offer ventilated designs specifically suited for warm conditions.

Can I wear ankle weights for long distance walking?

You can wear ankle weights for long distance walks, but keep them under 3 pounds and limit use to 2-3 times weekly to prevent repetitive strain injuries. Focus on shorter distances first to build tolerance before extending your walks.
