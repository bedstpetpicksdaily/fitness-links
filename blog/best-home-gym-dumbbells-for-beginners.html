  • Fixed-Weight Dumbbells in "Starter Sets": Budget sets with dumbbells stuck at 3, 5, and 8 pounds seem economical but force you to buy additional sets within weeks. You'll end up spending more money and cluttering your space—the opposite of what beginners need.
