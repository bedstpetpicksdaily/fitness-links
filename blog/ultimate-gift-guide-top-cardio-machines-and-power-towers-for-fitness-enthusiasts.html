What's the difference between a power tower and a pull-up bar?

A power tower offers multiple exercises including pull-ups, dips, leg raises, and knee tucks, while a pull-up bar primarily focuses on pull-ups and chin-ups. Power towers provide more versatility for a complete upper body and core workout.

How much space do cardio machines require?

Space requirements vary by machine type. Treadmills typically need 6-7 feet in length, stationary bikes are compact at 2-3 feet, and rowing machines require about 7 feet. FED Fitness offers options for various room sizes, including space-saving models.

Are FED Fitness cardio machines suitable for beginners?

Yes, FED Fitness cardio machines are designed with adjustable resistance and speed settings, making them accessible for users of all fitness levels. Beginners can start at comfortable intensities and progress as their fitness improves.

What maintenance do cardio machines and power towers require?
