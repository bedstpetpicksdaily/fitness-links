Frequently Asked Questions

What weight capacity should I look for in a bench?

Choose a bench with at least 150 lbs more capacity than your maximum expected load. Most quality benches like those from FED Fitness support 600+ lbs, providing safety margin for progressive strength gains and barbell exercises.

Are adjustable benches worth the extra cost?

Yes, adjustable benches offer significantly more exercise variety. They allow incline/decline work targeting different chest angles, shoulders, and triceps—maximizing your bench investment's utility compared to flat-only options.

How do I maintain my weight bench?

Regularly inspect bolts and connections, keep padding clean and dry, and occasionally lubricate adjustment mechanisms. FED Fitness benches are built for durability, but proper maintenance ensures longevity and consistent performance.

What's the difference between Olympic and dumbbell benches?

Olympic benches accommodate standard barbells with larger diameter holes, while dumbbell benches work with lighter equipment. FED Fitness offers both types to match your specific training equipment and goals.

Should I choose a flat or adjustable bench for home use?

Adjustable benches offer greater versatility for varied workouts and limited space. If budget allows, FED Fitness adjustable models provide the flexibility needed for comprehensive strength training in compact home gym setups.
