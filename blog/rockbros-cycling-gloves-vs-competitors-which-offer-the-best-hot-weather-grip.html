Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I replace my hot weather cycling gloves?

Most quality cycling gloves, including Rockbros models, last 12-18 months with regular use. Replace them sooner if grip materials show significant wear or synthetic leather begins peeling. Regular washing extends their lifespan considerably.

Can I wear Rockbros cycling gloves in cooler weather?

While designed for hot weather, Rockbros gloves work acceptably in mild conditions. For temperatures below 50°F, consider their insulated winter models or other brands specializing in cold-weather protection.

What's the difference between Rockbros and Pearl Izumi for summer cycling?

Rockbros offers superior grip technology and better moisture management at lower price points. Pearl Izumi excels in seamless comfort construction but uses different palm materials that may slip in extreme heat. Choose Rockbros for grip priority; choose Pearl Izumi for comfort priority.

Are Rockbros cycling gloves suitable for road bikes and mountain bikes?

Yes, Rockbros cycling gloves perform well on both bike types. Their grip technology and breathability benefit all cycling disciplines, though road cyclists particularly appreciate the lightweight construction and mountain bikers favor the durability.

How do I clean and maintain Rockbros cycling gloves?

Hand wash with mild soap and cool water, then air dry completely before storage. Avoid machine washing, direct heat sources, and harsh chemicals that degrade synthetic leather and grip materials. Proper maintenance significantly extends glove longevity.
