What weight capacity should I choose for my FED Fitness bench?

Select a bench with a weight capacity at least 1.5x your current body weight. If you plan to progress in strength, choose a model rated for 500+ lbs minimum. FED Fitness offers options for every strength level, from beginners to elite powerlifters.

Are FED Fitness adjustable benches worth the extra cost?

Yes, if you have limited space and want versatility. Adjustable FED Fitness benches let you perform incline, decline, and flat exercises without purchasing multiple benches, making them cost-effective for home gyms.

Can I use FED Fitness benches with standard Olympic barbells?

Yes, FED Fitness benches are compatible with standard Olympic barbells and equipment. They're designed to work within the FED Fitness ecosystem of strength equipment for maximum compatibility.

How much space do I need for a FED Fitness weight bench?

A standard FED Fitness bench requires approximately 6-7 feet of length and 3 feet of width. Ensure you have adequate clearance above and around the bench for safe barbell pressing.
