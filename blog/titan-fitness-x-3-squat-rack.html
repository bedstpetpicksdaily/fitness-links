Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can the Titan X-3 actually handle?

The X-3 is rated for approximately 800-1000 lbs depending on configuration, which is more than sufficient for 99% of home lifters. The real question is whether you'll ever actually load it that heavy. Most reviewers use it comfortably in the 400-600 lb range for serious working sets. The frame handles it without flexing, which is what matters in practice.

Is assembly really as difficult as reviews suggest?

Yes, expect it to be more complex than the marketing photos imply. You're essentially bolting together multiple steel sections with dozens of connection points. One person can technically do it, but two people make it dramatically easier. Give yourself 2-3 hours and have an impact wrench available. The instruction manual is adequate but not detailed enough for step-by-step visual learners.

How does the X-3 compare to other budget squat racks in July 2026?

The X-3 holds its own against comparable options like the Rogue RML-390F at lower price points, though Rogue racks generally have better resale value and accessory compatibility. If you're choosing between the X-3 and significantly cheaper Amazon-brand racks with 2-3 star ratings, the X-3 is worth the extra investment. If you're torn between the X-3 and used commercial equipment locally, inspect used options carefully for hidden damage or wear.
