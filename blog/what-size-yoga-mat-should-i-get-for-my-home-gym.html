What Size Yoga Mat Should I Get for My Home Gym?

A standard 68-inch long by 24-inch wide yoga mat is ideal for most home gyms and fits the average person with room to move. However, the right size depends on your height, available space, and the types of exercises you'll be doing.

The Short Answer

Standard yoga mats measure 68 inches long by 24 inches wide and work perfectly for most people and home gym spaces. If you're taller than 5'10", consider a 72 or 84-inch mat for full-body stretching without your head or feet hanging off. Measure your ceiling height and floor space before purchasing, as a mat that's too large will be awkward to store and use in a small room.

The Full Explanation

Standard Mat Dimensions

The most common yoga mat size is 68 inches (approximately 5.67 feet) long and 24 inches wide. This size accommodates the average person's height and provides enough width for side-to-side movements during most yoga poses and exercises. A standard mat is also the most affordable option and easiest to store in a home gym setup.

Height Considerations

Your height is the primary factor when choosing mat length. If you're shorter than 5'6", a standard 68-inch mat provides more than enough space. For those between 5'6" and 5'10", a standard mat still works well with a few inches to spare. If you're taller than 5'10", upgrade to a 72-inch or 84-inch mat to ensure your entire body fits comfortably when lying down or doing forward folds.

Space and Storage

Before buying, measure the space where you'll use and store your mat. A 68-inch mat requires a 5.67-foot by 2-foot area, which fits most home gym corners or spare rooms. If space is limited, a standard mat is your best option. Longer mats like 84-inch versions need dedicated space and are harder to roll up and store in closets or under beds.

Exercise Type Matters

Yoga mats in home gyms serve multiple purposes beyond yoga—they're used for stretching, core work, resistance band exercises, and general floor exercises. A standard 24-inch width is sufficient for most of these activities. However, if you plan to do side-lying exercises or partner workouts, you might prefer a wider 26 or 29-inch mat for extra stability and space.

Thickness Factors

While length and width are important, thickness also matters for comfort and stability. Most yoga mats range from 3mm to 8mm thick. A 4-6mm mat provides good cushioning for home gym use without making balance poses unstable. Thicker mats (6-8mm) offer more joint protection but take up more storage space.

What the Experts Say

Fitness professionals recommend matching mat length to your height with at least 6-12 inches of extra space. The International Association of Yoga recommends a standard mat for general fitness use, with longer options only necessary for people significantly above average height. Physical therapists suggest a 68-inch mat with 4-6mm thickness as the optimal choice for home gym versatility, since it works for yoga, stretching, and floor exercises without being cumbersome.
