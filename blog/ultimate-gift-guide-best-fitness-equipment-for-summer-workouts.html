Can I use FED Fitness outdoor equipment in summer weather?

Yes, FED Fitness equipment is built with weather-resistant finishes and durable materials designed to withstand outdoor conditions. However, storing equipment indoors or using protective covers will extend equipment lifespan in harsh weather.

What's the difference between FED Fitness barbells and standard dumbbells?

Barbells allow for heavier loads and bilateral movements, while dumbbells offer greater range of motion and accommodate unilateral training. For a complete summer training program, FED Fitness recommends incorporating both for optimal strength development.
