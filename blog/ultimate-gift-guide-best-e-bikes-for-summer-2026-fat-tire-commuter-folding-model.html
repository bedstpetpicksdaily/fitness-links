Frequently Asked Questions

Are e-bikes suitable for all fitness levels?

Yes. E-bikes feature adjustable pedal-assist levels, allowing riders of any fitness level to enjoy cycling. You control the motor's assistance, making rides easier or more challenging based on your preferences and abilities.

How far can an e-bike travel on a single charge?

Range varies by model, battery capacity, terrain, and rider weight. Most Burchda Bikes e-bikes offer 30-60 miles per charge. Fat tire and commuter models typically provide 40-50 miles, while some premium models exceed 60 miles.

What's the weight difference between regular bikes and e-bikes?

E-bikes are heavier due to motors and batteries, typically weighing 45-65 pounds compared to 25-30 pounds for traditional bikes. However, modern designs like Burchda's folding models minimize weight while maintaining performance.

Do I need a special license or helmet to ride an e-bike?

Requirements vary by location. Most places don't require special licenses for e-bikes under specific wattage and speed limits. Always check local regulations. Wearing a helmet is strongly recommended for safety, regardless of legal requirements.

How often should I maintain my e-bike?

E-bikes require standard bicycle maintenance: regular tire checks, brake inspections, chain lubrication, and periodic professional tune-ups. Battery maintenance involves proper charging practices and occasional cleaning of electrical connections.
