Building Your Summer Cardio Program

To see dramatic results before summer, commit to 4-5 rebounder cardio sessions weekly. Week one: focus on building endurance with 15-20 minute sessions of steady-paced bouncing. Week three: combine movement variations—alternate between high knees, side bounces, and twist movements. Week four: push intensity with longer intervals and decreased rest periods.
