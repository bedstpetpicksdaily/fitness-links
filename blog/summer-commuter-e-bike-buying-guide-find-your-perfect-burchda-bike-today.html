Frequently Asked Questions

How far can a Burchda e-bike travel on a single charge?

Burchda e-bikes typically offer 30-60 miles of range per charge, depending on the model, battery capacity, terrain, and assistance level used. Commuter models are optimized for efficiency, while fat tire bikes may have slightly lower range due to added weight and terrain demands. Always check individual product specifications for exact range details.

Are Burchda folding e-bikes suitable for serious commuting?

Yes, absolutely. Burchda's folding e-bike models are specifically engineered for commuters who need multi-modal transportation flexibility. They feature sturdy frames, reliable motors, and adequate battery capacity for daily use while remaining compact enough to store in apartments, offices, or vehicles.

What's the difference between a Burchda commuter e-bike and fat tire model?

Commuter e-bikes prioritize lightweight design, speed, and efficiency for urban streets and paved paths. Fat tire e-bikes feature wider, more durable tires and robust suspension systems, making them ideal for off-road trails, sand, and rough terrain. Choose based on where you'll primarily ride.

Do Burchda e-bikes require special licensing or registration?

Licensing requirements vary by location. Most areas classify e-bikes with motors under 750W as regular bicycles requiring no special registration. However, check your local laws, as some regions have specific e-bike classifications and requirements.

How long do Burchda e-bike batteries typically last?

Quality Burchda batteries are engineered to retain 80% capacity after 500-1000 charge cycles, translating to 3-5+ years of typical use. Proper storage, regular maintenance, and avoiding extreme temperatures extend battery lifespan significantly.
