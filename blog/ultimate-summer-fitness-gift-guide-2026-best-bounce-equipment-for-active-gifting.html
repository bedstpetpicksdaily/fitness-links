Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a mini trampoline and a full-sized rebounder?

Mini trampolines are more compact and portable, ideal for apartment living and storage. Full-sized rebounders offer more bounce surface area and advanced stability features for intensive workouts. Boogie Bounce offers both options depending on user needs and available space.

Is bounce fitness equipment suitable for beginners?

Absolutely. Boogie Bounce equipment is designed for all fitness levels. Beginners can start with low-intensity bouncing and gradually increase intensity as their balance and endurance improve. The low-impact nature makes it particularly safe for those new to fitness.

Can bounce equipment provide effective cardio workouts?

Yes. Rebounding is an excellent cardiovascular workout that elevates heart rate, improves circulation, and builds endurance. Boogie Bounce rebounders are engineered to maximize cardio benefits while maintaining joint safety, making them comparable to or superior to traditional cardio machines.

How much space do I need for a Boogie Bounce rebounder?

Mini trampolines require minimal space—as little as 4x4 feet. Full-sized rebounders need approximately 6x6 feet of floor space with at least 8-9 feet of ceiling clearance for safe bouncing. Always ensure adequate space before purchasing.

Are Boogie Bounce products durable and worth the investment?

Boogie Bounce is known for high-quality construction and durability. Their equipment is built to withstand regular use and maintain consistent bounce quality over time, making them excellent long-term fitness investments that provide years of reliable performance.
