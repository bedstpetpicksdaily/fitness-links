Yes, Boogie Bounce rebounders are designed for all fitness levels. Beginners can start with gentle bouncing and gradually increase intensity as they build balance and coordination.

How often can I use a Boogie Bounce rebounder for cardio training?

The low-impact nature of rebounders allows for frequent use. Most people can safely use Boogie Bounce equipment 4-6 times per week, though rest days are still important for recovery.
