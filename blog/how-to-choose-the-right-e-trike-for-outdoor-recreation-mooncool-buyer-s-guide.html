Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between an e-trike and an e-bike?

E-trikes have three wheels providing superior stability and balance, making them ideal for seniors and those with mobility concerns. E-bikes are two-wheeled and require more balancing skill. Mooncool's e-trikes excel in stability, comfort, and load capacity.

How long do e-trike batteries typically last?

Most quality e-trike batteries, including those in Mooncool models, last 3-5 years with proper care. Battery life depends on usage frequency and charging habits. Mooncool batteries are built for durability and include comprehensive warranties.

Are Mooncool e-trikes suitable for seniors?

Yes, absolutely. Mooncool specifically designs many models for seniors and older adults, featuring low step-through frames, comfortable seating, responsive brakes, and gentle pedal-assist that reduces strain while keeping riders active.

What's the typical range of a Mooncool e-trike?

Mooncool e-trikes typically offer 30-60 miles of range per full charge, depending on the model, rider weight, terrain, and assist level used. This makes them perfect for extended outdoor recreational rides.

Do I need a license to ride an e-trike?

In most U.S. states, e-trikes with motors under 750W and speeds under 20 mph don't require a license. However, regulations vary by location. Always check your local laws before purchasing. Mooncool's models comply with standard regulations in most areas.
