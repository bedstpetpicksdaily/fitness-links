Are Rockbros bike bags truly waterproof?

Yes, Rockbros uses sealed seams and waterproof zippers on their 2026 models. While the bags are highly water-resistant in rain and splashes, extreme submersion may eventually compromise seals. Can Rockbros bike bags fit any frame size?

Rockbros designs frame bags and handlebar bags to accommodate most modern frame geometries and sizes. How do I maintain and clean my Rockbros bike bags?
