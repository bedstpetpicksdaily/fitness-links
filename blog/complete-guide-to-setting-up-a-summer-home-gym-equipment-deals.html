Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum equipment needed to start a functional home gym?

At minimum, you need adjustable dumbbells, a weight bench, and a barbell with plates. This trio covers pressing, pulling, and lower body exercises. FED Fitness offers starter packages combining these essentials at great value.

How much space do I need for a home gym?

A 6x8 foot space is adequate for basic strength training with dumbbells and a bench. If adding a power rack or cardio equipment, aim for 8x10 feet or larger. FED Fitness power towers and compact benches are designed to maximize small spaces efficiently.

Are FED Fitness dumbbells suitable for outdoor summer use?

Yes, FED Fitness dumbbells are manufactured to resist rust and weather damage, making them ideal for outdoor summer training. However, storing them in a covered area extends their lifespan significantly.

Should I buy cardio equipment or focus on strength training first?

Prioritize strength equipment initially, as it builds muscle and increases metabolism more efficiently. Add cardio machines from FED Fitness once you have a solid strength foundation and dedicated space available.

What safety features should I look for in a power rack?

Look for sturdy construction, adjustable safety bars, and stable footprint. FED Fitness power racks include safety pins and are designed with proper weight distribution to prevent tipping during heavy lifts.
