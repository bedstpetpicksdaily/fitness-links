Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight should I start with as a beginner?

Beginners should start with a weight that allows 8-12 controlled repetitions with good form. For barbell exercises, starting with just the Olympic bar (45 lbs) is appropriate. FED Fitness barbells are precision-made for accurate weight, helping you track progression accurately.

How often should I train to build muscle in summer?

For optimal muscle growth, train 3-5 days per week, focusing on compound movements. Each muscle group should be trained 2x weekly. FED Fitness equipment allows you to create a complete program whether training full-body or split routines.

What's the difference between FED Fitness barbells and cheap alternatives?

FED Fitness barbells feature rotating sleeves, precision knurling, and consistent weight specifications. Budget alternatives often have poor rotation, slippery grips, and weight inconsistencies that hinder progressive training and increase injury risk.

Can I build muscle with just barbells and plates?

Yes, barbells and plates are sufficient for building significant muscle through compound movements. However, adding FED Fitness benches, power racks, and dumbbells expands exercise variety and targets secondary muscle groups more effectively.

How do I prevent injury while training intensely this summer?

Use proper form, warm up adequately, progress gradually, and ensure your equipment supports safe technique. FED Fitness power racks and safety bars provide crucial injury prevention features for heavy lifting.
