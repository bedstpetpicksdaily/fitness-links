Adjustable dumbbells save space and allow progressive overload as your strength increases, making them ideal for home setups with limited room. FED Fitness offers a comprehensive range of dumbbell sets with quality construction, ensuring your investment lasts through years of training.

A basic home gym with dumbbells, a bench, and power tower requires approximately 8x10 feet. FED Fitness equipment is designed to fit various spaces, from small apartments to dedicated garage gyms. Vertical storage and compact designs maximize efficiency.

Are adjustable dumbbells better than fixed dumbbells?

Both have advantages. Adjustable dumbbells save space and money initially, while fixed dumbbells are more durable for heavy lifting. FED Fitness offers quality options in both categories, allowing you to choose based on your space and budget.

Power racks suit serious lifters performing heavy compound lifts, while power towers work for functional strength and bodyweight exercises in limited spaces. FED Fitness offers both, so choose based on your goals and available space.
