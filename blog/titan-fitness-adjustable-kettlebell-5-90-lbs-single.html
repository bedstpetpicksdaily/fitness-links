Is the dial locking mechanism reliable long-term?

After 90+ days of consistent use—roughly 4 workouts weekly—I haven't experienced any slippage or mechanical degradation. The locking mechanism is mechanically simple (a virtue, not a flaw). The bigger question is whether 500+ existing customer reviews reveal long-term issues. Most complaints focus on initial shipping damage, not mechanical failure, which suggests durability isn't a hidden problem.

Will this kettlebell work for ballistic movements like snatches and cleans?

Yes, but expect a 2-3 week adaptation period. The wider base and higher center of gravity compared to traditional kettlebells change the biomechanics slightly. If you're training for kettlebell sport specifically, a traditional cast iron bell might feel more natural.
