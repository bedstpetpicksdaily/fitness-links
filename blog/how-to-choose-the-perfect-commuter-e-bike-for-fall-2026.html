Frequently Asked Questions

What range should a commuter e-bike have?

Most commuters need 40-80 miles per charge. If your daily round trip is 20-40 miles, aim for at least 60 miles of range to account for hills, cold weather, and battery degradation over time.

Are folding electric bikes as reliable as full-frame commuter e-bikes?

Yes, quality folding electric bikes are reliable and durable. Burchda Bikes' folding models use sturdy hinges and frames designed for daily commuting. Choose folding if portability matters; choose full-frame if maximum stability is your priority.

Can fat tire electric bikes handle wet fall weather?

Absolutely. Fat tires provide superior grip and traction on wet, slippery surfaces. They're excellent for fall commuting when roads are wet from rain or damp leaves.

How often should I charge my commuter e-bike?

Charge after each commute if your range is tight, or every 2-3 commutes if you have surplus range. Most modern batteries, including those in Burchda Bikes e-bikes, handle frequent charging without degradation.
