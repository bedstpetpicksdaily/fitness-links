Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Titan X-3 compare to cheaper squat racks under $300?

Cheaper racks often develop wobble after 6-12 months of regular use, shift when you load heavy, or require constant readjustment of pins. The X-3's higher price reflects materials that hold up to repeated heavy loading. Over 3-5 years, paying more upfront saves frustration and ultimately money versus replacing a budget rack twice.

Can you actually do bench press safely in this cage?

Yes, with proper setup. The adjustable spotter arms are the key feature here — you can set them so they catch the bar if you fail a rep. This matters for solo trainers. Just verify ceiling clearance before ordering; tall users sometimes find the uprights interfere with full range of motion on incline presses.

Is assembly really as quick as advertised?

Plan for 45-60 minutes, not 20. It's straightforward if you have a power drill and follow the manual in order, but rushing causes misalignment. Pro tip: July is actually ideal timing to assemble since you've got daylight and less weather stress; assemble outdoors and move inside once complete.

Will this fit in a standard 1-car garage?

It depends on your garage size and what else you're storing. Measure the footprint (roughly 4x3 feet) and account for at least 3 extra feet of clearance in front for barbell movement and dumbbells or resistance bands nearby. Many owners fit it, but it requires intentional space planning.

What's the warranty like if something breaks?

Titan typically offers 1-year warranties on structural components; check your specific purchase details. The good news: with 500+ reviews averaging 4.3 stars, catastrophic failures are rare. Most owners report zero issues after years of use, which is the best warranty.
