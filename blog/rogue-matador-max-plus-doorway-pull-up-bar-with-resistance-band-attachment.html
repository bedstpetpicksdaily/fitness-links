Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be able to do pull-ups already to use the resistance bands effectively?

Absolutely not. That's the entire point. If you can't do even one pull-up, start with a heavy resistance band (typically the thickest one available). It supports significant bodyweight—often 60-100+ pounds depending on the band. Over 3-4 weeks of consistent work, you reduce band thickness as you get stronger. I've seen this work for people starting from complete zero pull-up ability.

Will the doorway bar damage my door frame or walls?

The Matador uses pressure-based mounting rather than drilling, so zero permanent damage. The padded contact points protect the frame. I've tested it on painted doorframes, stained wood, and drywall edges—no marks or issues. It does press into the frame with significant force when you're hanging, so fragile doorways (like those in extremely old homes) might need a consultation, but standard modern doorways handle it fine.

How often do the resistance bands that come with it actually break or lose elasticity?

The bands included aren't Rogue's premium rubber—they're functional and durable but not competition-grade. Mine have held strong for two months of regular use (typically 4-5 sessions weekly), with zero loss of resistance or visible wear. Most users report 6-12 months of reliable use before replacement becomes necessary. Replacement bands are inexpensive and widely available, so it's not a hidden cost issue.

Can I attach other resistance bands or equipment to this bar beyond what comes included?

The attachment system is proprietary to Rogue's design, so you can't just throw any band at it. However, the system is versatile enough that most standard resistance bands work if you get creative with looping. I've successfully used non-Rogue bands by routing them through the designated attachment points. It's not as seamless as the included setup, but it expands your options without replacing the bar.
