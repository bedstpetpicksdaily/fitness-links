Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Titan X-3 compare to the REP Fitness PR-4000 at double the price?

The PR-4000 uses premium 11-gauge steel and has slightly better finish quality, but functionally they're closer than the price difference suggests. For 90% of home lifters, the X-3 delivers 95% of the performance at 50% of the cost. The PR-4000 is worth it only if you're training 15+ years and want museum-quality equipment. For your first serious rack? X-3 wins.

Can I actually use the pull-up bar for heavy weighted pull-ups?

Yes, and this is where the X-3 separates itself from budget competitors. The integrated design eliminates the wobble you get with bolt-on attachments. Users consistently report feeling confident at 50+ lbs added weight. The bar diameter is 1.25 inches—thick enough for serious grip work without being uncomfortable for daily training.

Is the X-3 better than DIY pipe racks or the Rogue R-3?

DIY pipes cost less but require welding skills, time, and yield a less refined product. The Rogue R-3 costs $400-600 more and offers marginally better finish; the Titan X-3 is the practical middle ground. Pick Rogue only if budget is irrelevant. Pick Titan if you want professional-grade equipment without premium pricing.
