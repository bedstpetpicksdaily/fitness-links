Hot yoga classes present unique challenges for your mat that standard yoga practice doesn't. When you're sweating heavily in 90-105 degree rooms, your mat needs to handle moisture, provide superior grip, and maintain its integrity through intense heat exposure. Choosing the right mat can be the difference between a safe, focused practice and constantly slipping, sliding, and worrying about your equipment falling apart.

What to Look For

  • Superior Grip and Moisture Absorption – Look for mats with textured surfaces or natural rubber construction that actually grips better when wet, rather than becoming slippery. Your hands and feet need reliable traction even as sweat accumulates during your flow.
  • Heat-Resistant Materials – Choose mats made from high-quality natural rubber, PER (Polymer Environmental Resin), or specialized synthetic blends that won't degrade, off-gas, or become tacky in high temperatures. Cheap PVC mats often break down faster and release unpleasant odors when heated.
  • Quick-Drying and Antimicrobial Properties – Hot yoga means heavy sweating, which creates an ideal environment for bacteria and mold growth. Select mats that dry quickly and have natural antimicrobial properties, or that you can easily clean and sanitize between sessions.
