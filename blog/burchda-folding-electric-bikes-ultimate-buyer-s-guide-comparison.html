Are Burchda folding bikes suitable for tall riders?

Most Burchda folding e-bikes feature adjustable seatposts and handlebars to accommodate riders of various heights. Check specific model specifications, as weight capacity and frame geometry vary. Generally, riders up to 6'2" find comfortable fit options.

What's the weight limit for Burchda electric bikes?

Most Burchda models support riders up to 250-300 lbs, including cargo. Fat tire models typically handle higher weight limits. Always verify the specific model's weight capacity before purchase, as specifications vary.

How do I maintain my Burchda folding e-bike?

Regular maintenance includes checking tire pressure, lubricating the chain, inspecting brakes, and keeping the battery charged. Burchda provides detailed maintenance guides with each purchase. Professional servicing is recommended annually for optimal performance.
