Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a rebounder and a regular trampoline?

Rebounders (mini trampolines) are smaller, designed for stationary cardio fitness with tight, controlled bounce mechanics. Regular trampolines are larger recreational equipment built for high-impact jumping and aerial tricks. Rebounders prioritize joint safety and cardiovascular benefit; trampolines prioritize flight and fun.

How often should I use a rebounder for best fitness results?

For measurable cardiovascular improvement, aim for 20–30 minutes of bouncing 3–4 times weekly. Beginners should start with 2–3 sessions per week to allow your stabilizer muscles to adapt. Daily use is safe once you've built baseline conditioning.

Can I leave my rebounder outside year-round?

Not recommended. Extended outdoor exposure degrades the mat, springs, and frame. Store rebounders indoors during winter months, or cover them with weather-resistant tarps if storage isn't available. This extends equipment lifespan significantly.

Is a rebounder suitable for beginners?

Yes. Rebounding is low-impact and accessible for all fitness levels. Begin with slower bouncing to establish balance and coordination, then gradually increase intensity. Boogie Bounce specifically manufactures entry-level models designed for beginners.

How much outdoor space do I need for a rebounder?

Full-sized models need roughly 8–10 feet of clearance around them. Compact rebounders require 5–6 feet. Measure your patio or yard dimensions before purchasing. Ensure overhead clearance if you bounce near low-hanging branches or structures.
