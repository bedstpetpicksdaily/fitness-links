Frequently Asked Questions

Are Mooncool electric tricycles suitable for seniors?

Yes, Mooncool designs their e-trikes specifically with seniors in mind. The three-wheel stability, comfortable seating, and pedal-assist features make them ideal for older adults seeking accessible outdoor mobility without excessive physical strain.

How far can a Mooncool e-trike travel on a single charge?

Most Mooncool electric tricycles offer 30-50+ miles of range per charge, depending on the model, riding mode, terrain, and rider weight. Pedal-assist mode typically extends range compared to full throttle use.

Do I need a license or registration for a Mooncool electric tricycle?

Regulations vary by location and depend on motor power and speed capabilities. Most e-trikes with motors under 750W are treated as bicycles and don't require licensing in many areas. Always check local regulations before purchasing.

What's the weight capacity of Mooncool e-trikes?

Most Mooncool models support riders up to 300-330 lbs, though capacity varies by specific model. Check individual product specifications to ensure your e-trike meets your weight requirements.

How long does the Mooncool battery last before replacement?

Mooncool batteries typically last 2-4 years with proper maintenance, translating to 1,000+ charge cycles. Battery lifespan depends on usage patterns, charging habits, and environmental conditions.
