Frequently Asked Questions

Are Rockbros bike bags waterproof?

Yes, Rockbros bike bags feature premium waterproof materials and sealed seams designed to keep your belongings dry during rainy summer rides. Most models are rated for heavy rain and splash resistance, though submerging them isn't recommended.

Can Rockbros handlebar bags fit on all bike types?

Rockbros handlebar bags are designed with universal mounting systems that work with most modern bikes, including road bikes, mountain bikes, and hybrids. However, it's recommended to check compatibility with your specific handlebar diameter before purchasing.

What's the best Rockbros bag capacity for summer day rides?

For day rides, Rockbros handlebar bags with 1.5-2L capacity are ideal, providing enough space for phone, keys, wallet, and snacks without excess weight. For longer adventures, consider 3-5L frame bags or larger capacity solutions.

How do I clean and maintain my Rockbros bike bag?

Wipe Rockbros bags with a damp cloth after rides, especially after rain or dusty trails. For deeper cleaning, use mild soap and water. Ensure bags dry completely before storage to prevent mold and maintain the waterproof coating's integrity.
