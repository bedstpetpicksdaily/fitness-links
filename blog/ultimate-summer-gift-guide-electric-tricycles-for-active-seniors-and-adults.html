Frequently Asked Questions

Are electric tricycles safe for seniors?

Yes, absolutely. Electric tricycles offer enhanced safety compared to traditional bikes due to their three-point stability, low center of gravity, and responsive braking systems. Mooncool models are specifically designed with senior safety in mind, featuring intuitive controls and ergonomic seating.

How far can a Mooncool electric tricycle travel on a single charge?

Range varies by model and riding conditions, but most Mooncool e-trikes offer between 30-60 miles per charge. Factors like rider weight, terrain, motor assistance level, and weather conditions affect actual range. Check specific model specifications on the Mooncool website for detailed range information.

What's the difference between an electric tricycle and an electric bicycle?

Electric tricycles feature three wheels for enhanced stability and typically have larger cargo capacity, making them ideal for seniors and riders prioritizing safety. E-bikes have two wheels and require better balance. Tricycles are more comfortable for extended rides and recreational use.

How long does it take to charge a Mooncool e-trike?

Most Mooncool electric tricycles charge fully in 4-6 hours using a standard household outlet. Fast-charging options may be available for specific models. Always refer to your model's manual for precise charging times and recommendations.

Can I ride an electric tricycle in the rain?

Yes, Mooncool e-trikes are built with weather-resistant components suitable for light rain. However, avoid heavy downpours and ensure proper maintenance. Always wear appropriate safety gear and exercise caution on wet surfaces, as traction may be reduced.
