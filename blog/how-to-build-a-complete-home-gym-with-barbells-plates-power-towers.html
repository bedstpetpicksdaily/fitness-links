Start with Quality Barbells and Weight Plates

The barbell is the cornerstone of any serious strength training program. When selecting a barbell for your home gym, consider the shaft diameter, knurling pattern, and weight capacity. Olympic barbells (45 lbs for men, 35 lbs for women) are the standard and work for most lifters. FED Fitness offers premium barbells with aggressive knurling for secure grip and smooth rotation, ensuring consistent performance across all lifting styles.

Equally important are your weight plates. Quality plates maintain consistency in weight distribution and durability over thousands of lifts. FED Fitness provides cast iron and bumper plates in all denominations, allowing you to scale your workouts progressively. Start with a basic selection—25s, 10s, and 5s—then expand as your strength increases.

Invest in Power Racks and Power Towers

A power rack provides safety, versatility, and enables heavier lifting without a spotter. Power racks accommodate squats, bench presses, overhead presses, and more. FED Fitness power racks come with adjustable safety arms and pull-up bars, maximizing functionality in limited space. If you're short on room, FED Fitness power towers offer an excellent alternative—combining strength training with bodyweight exercises like dips, pull-ups, and leg raises in a compact footprint.

Complete Your Setup with Benches and Accessories

A quality weight bench transforms your barbell into a complete upper-body tool. Adjustable benches allow flat, incline, and decline positions for varied chest, shoulder, and tricep work. FED Fitness weight benches feature reinforced construction, comfortable padding, and stability for heavy pressing. Pair your bench with dumbbells for accessory work, and consider adding cardio machines like rowing machines or stationary bikes to round out your program and maintain cardiovascular fitness.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much space do I need for a home gym?

A basic home gym requires 100-200 square feet. A power rack with barbell and bench needs roughly 8x10 feet. Power towers are more compact, fitting in 4x6 feet of floor space. FED Fitness offers space-efficient designs that maximize functionality in smaller areas.

What's the ideal starting weight for barbells and plates?

Beginners should start with a barbell (45 lbs) and 100-150 lbs of total plate weight. This allows practice of proper form before progression. Most lifters find 200-300 lbs of plates adequate for home training. FED Fitness plates are clearly labeled and offered in all denominations for easy progression.

Are power towers or power racks better for home use?

Power racks offer more versatility for barbell work and heavier lifts, while power towers excel at bodyweight exercises and take up less space. Ideally, both complement each other. If choosing one, consider your primary training goals. FED Fitness manufactures both, helping you find the perfect fit for your space and objectives.

How often should I replace home gym equipment?

Quality strength equipment lasts decades with proper care. Barbells and plates are virtually indestructible. Benches and racks may need occasional maintenance. FED Fitness products are built for longevity, making them excellent long-term investments in your home fitness setup.
