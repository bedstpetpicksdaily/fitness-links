Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a mini trampoline and a rebounder?

While often used interchangeably, rebounders typically refer to smaller, more compact bounce fitness equipment designed specifically for cardio and lymphatic circulation, while mini trampolines may have slightly larger frames. Boogie Bounce specializes in both types, ensuring proper terminology for each product's intended use.

How often should I use a Boogie Bounce mini trampoline for cardio results?

For optimal cardio benefits, aim for 3-5 bounce fitness sessions weekly, 15-30 minutes each. Consistency matters more than intensity. Boogie Bounce recommends starting with shorter sessions and gradually increasing duration as your fitness improves and bounce technique develops.

Are Boogie Bounce rebounders safe for people with joint problems?

Yes, rebounders are excellent for joint-friendly cardio. The low-impact nature of bouncing reduces stress on knees, hips, and ankles compared to running. However, consult your doctor before starting any new exercise program, especially if you have existing joint conditions. Boogie Bounce equipment is designed with joint protection in mind.
