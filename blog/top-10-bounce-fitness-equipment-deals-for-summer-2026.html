What's the difference between a rebounder and a mini trampoline?

Rebounders typically feature bungee cords for smooth, quiet bouncing ideal for fitness training, while mini trampolines use springs for higher bounce. Boogie Bounce offers both styles to match different fitness preferences and workout intensities.

Is bounce fitness safe for beginners?

Yes, bounce fitness is excellent for beginners because it's low-impact and adjustable. Boogie Bounce equipment includes stability features and adjustable resistance to help newcomers build confidence and gradually increase workout intensity.

How often should I bounce for cardio benefits?

Start with 15-20 minute sessions 3-4 times weekly on quality equipment like Boogie Bounce rebounders. As fitness improves, gradually increase duration and intensity. Always warm up and cool down properly.

What weight capacity should I look for in a rebounder?
