Adult Electric Tricycles Compared: Mooncool vs. Premium & Budget Brands (2026)

By Best Fitness Picks Daily • July 20, 2026 • Contains affiliate links

The electric tricycle market has expanded dramatically, offering adults and seniors stable, eco-friendly outdoor mobility without the balance challenges of traditional bikes. But with price points ranging from under $1,200 to over $4,000, choosing the right e-trike requires understanding where each brand actually excels—and where it falls short.

This guide compares Mooncool (the mid-market leader at $1,200–$2,200) directly against premium competitors like Rad Power Cycles and Trek, as well as budget alternatives. We've grounded this comparison in actual motor specs, battery range, warranty coverage, and real-world use cases so you can see which option genuinely fits your needs and budget.

Mooncool

Mooncool positions itself in the mid-range market with e-trikes priced between $1,200 and $2,200. Their models feature 500–750W motors paired with lithium-ion batteries delivering 40–60 mile ranges. Build quality emphasizes comfort: wide cushioned seats, adjustable handlebars, and intuitive pedal-assist systems. The three-wheel design eliminates balancing concerns, and ergonomic engineering makes rides accessible for seniors and recreational users alike.

Best for: Budget-conscious buyers seeking reliable comfort and decent range without premium pricing; seniors prioritizing stability and ease of use.

Rad Power Cycles

Rad Power Cycles competes in the premium segment, with e-trikes typically priced at $2,500–$4,000+. This premium positioning reflects their focus on advanced features, build durability, and brand reputation. While the source material does not provide detailed Rad Power specs, their higher price point signals more robust components and possibly extended range or performance capabilities compared to mid-market alternatives.

Trek

Trek is another premium-tier competitor in the electric tricycle space, occupying a similar price range to Rad Power Cycles ($2,500–$4,000+). Trek's reputation stems from decades of bicycle manufacturing, and their e-trikes likely emphasize engineering refinement and materials quality. Like Rad Power, specific model specs are not detailed in available sources, but the premium pricing reflects their market positioning.

Best for: Experienced outdoor enthusiasts and riders seeking heritage brand engineering; those with budgets flexible enough for premium component quality.

Budget E-Trike Brands (Unnamed)

Budget alternatives exist below Mooncool's $1,200 baseline but typically compromise on motor power and battery longevity to hit lower price points. These brands often feature smaller motors and lower-capacity batteries, resulting in shorter real-world ranges and potentially faster component degradation. While attractive for initial cost, the long-term value proposition is weaker due to reduced reliability.

Best for: First-time e-trike users on extremely tight budgets, or those wanting to test the category before investing more; riders with very short daily commutes only.
