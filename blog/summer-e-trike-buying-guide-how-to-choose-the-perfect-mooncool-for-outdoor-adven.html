Battery Life, Charging, and Maintenance Tips

Understanding battery management ensures your Mooncool e-trike stays ready for summer fun. Modern lithium-ion batteries in Mooncool tricycles typically last 3-5 years with proper care. Charge your trike regularly—ideally before battery levels drop below 20%—and store it in cool, dry conditions during off-season months. Most Mooncool models charge fully in 4-8 hours using standard household outlets. Regular maintenance including tire pressure checks, brake inspections, and chain lubrication keeps your electric tricycle performing optimally throughout summer and beyond.
