What to Look For

  • Superior Grip When Wet: Look for mats with textured or rubberized surfaces that actually improve their grip as you sweat, rather than becoming slippery.   • Quick-Dry, Moisture-Resistant Materials: Mats made from rubber, PVC, or TPE (thermoplastic elastomer) dry faster and resist water damage far better than traditional PVC or foam. This matters whether you're wiping down sweat mid-session or storing the mat in a gym bag after class.
  • Lightweight and Compact Design: A truly portable mat should weigh under 3.5 pounds and compress to fit in a yoga bag or gym tote. If you're carrying it to hot yoga studios or studios, you'll appreciate a mat that doesn't add bulk to your routine.
