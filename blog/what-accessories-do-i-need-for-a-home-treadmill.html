The Short Answer

You don't need much to start using a treadmill, but a few key accessories make a significant difference in your workout experience. A treadmill mat protects your flooring and reduces noise, while a water bottle keeps you hydrated. A towel helps manage sweat, and a phone or tablet holder lets you watch entertainment or track your workout metrics. These four items cover the basics for most home treadmill users.

The Full Explanation

When setting up your home treadmill, think about three main categories: protection, comfort, and convenience.

Protection and Stability

A treadmill mat is perhaps the most important accessory you can buy. It protects your flooring from impact damage, reduces vibration noise that disturbs neighbors or family members, and provides a stable base that prevents your treadmill from shifting during use. Mats also make cleaning easier by containing dust and sweat. Look for mats that are at least 1/4 inch thick and made from durable rubber or foam materials.

Hydration and Comfort

A quality water bottle is non-negotiable for any workout routine. Staying hydrated improves performance and recovery. Many runners prefer insulated bottles that keep water cool during longer sessions. A microfiber towel specifically designed for workouts absorbs sweat efficiently and dries quickly, helping you stay comfortable and protecting your treadmill's console and handles from sweat damage.

Entertainment and Tracking

A phone or tablet holder transforms your treadmill sessions by letting you watch shows, videos, or fitness classes. It also keeps your phone accessible for tracking fitness apps or monitoring your metrics. Some holders are adjustable to fit various screen sizes, while others are specifically designed for particular devices.

Additional Helpful Accessories

Beyond the essentials, consider: a headphone or earbud holder for cable management, wrist or armband holders for phones if you prefer hands-free carrying, a spray bottle and cloth for cleaning the treadmill after workouts, and a heart rate monitor for more detailed fitness tracking. Desk stands or treadmill desks are popular for people who want to work while walking at lower speeds.

What the Experts Say

Fitness professionals emphasize that accessories should enhance your routine without complicating it. According to trainers and physical therapists, the treadmill mat is the single most recommended purchase because it protects both your investment and your home. Hydration experts stress that having water immediately accessible increases the likelihood you'll drink enough during workouts. Equipment designers note that device holders reduce the temptation to hold onto the treadmill console, which improves your natural gait and workout effectiveness. The general consensus is that basic accessories pay for themselves through equipment longevity and improved workout consistency.
