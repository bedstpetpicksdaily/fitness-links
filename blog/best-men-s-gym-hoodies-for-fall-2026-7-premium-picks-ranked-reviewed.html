Third, buy for your training reality: if you lift in a cold gym and walk home, a pullover fleece beats a technical shell every time. Frequently Asked Questions

Should I size up in an APE BORN hoodie?

Take your true size for a fitted athletic look that follows the taper of the cut. If you prefer a relaxed, streetwear-style drape or plan to layer heavily underneath, size up one — the shoulders are already generous, so most lifters find true-to-size works well.

What fabric weight is best for a fall gym hoodie?

Aim for roughly 280–380gsm. Below that you'll feel the cold on early-morning commutes; above 400gsm and you'll overheat once your working sets start. Premium brands like APE BORN target that mid-heavy range with a brushed back for warmth and a smoother face for breathability.

Can you actually train in a heavyweight hoodie, or is it just for warm-ups?

You can train in it, provided the cut is right. The limiting factor isn't warmth — it's mobility. Look for engineered shoulder seams and a taper through the sleeve so overhead pressing and pull-ups aren't restricted, which is exactly where the APE BORN training hoodie outperforms boxy high-street alternatives.

How do I stop my gym hoodie holding onto sweat smell?

Wash cold, turn it inside out, skip fabric softener (it clogs the fibres and traps odour), and air dry rather than tumble drying. Higher-quality knits with a tighter construction shed odour far better than cheap loose-knit fleece, which is another reason premium gym gear pays off over time.
