Without adequate enzyme activity, even the most nutrient-rich meals pass through your system incompletely absorbed.

Timing and Dosing for Maximum Effectiveness

The timing of digestive supplements is crucial. Taking them just before or with your largest meals ensures optimal enzyme activity when your digestive system needs it most. During harvest season when meals are often larger and more complex, starting with digestive support 15-30 minutes before eating gives your body preparation time. For harvest feasts with multiple courses, consider a second dose midway through the meal.
