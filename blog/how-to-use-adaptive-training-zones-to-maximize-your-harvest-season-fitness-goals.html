Understanding Adaptive Training Zones and Why They Matter

Traditional training zones rely on fixed percentages of your maximum heart rate, typically categorized as zones 1-5 ranging from recovery to maximum effort. This dynamic approach ensures you're always training at the intensity that delivers maximum results without overtraining or underutilizing your potential.
