Frequently Asked Questions

What size Rockbros handlebar bag do I need for long rides?

Rockbros offers multiple handlebar bag sizes. For long rides, choose a larger capacity bag (typically 2-3L) that can hold a phone, keys, snacks, and basic tools. Consider your specific route and essentials to determine the perfect size for your needs.

Are Rockbros bike bags waterproof?

Yes, most Rockbros bike bags feature waterproof or water-resistant materials designed to protect contents from rain and sweat during summer cycling. Always check product specifications for your specific bag model.

Can I use Rockbros handlebar bags on any bike type?

Rockbros handlebar bags are designed to work with most standard handlebar configurations. However, verify handlebar diameter compatibility and mounting system compatibility with your specific bike before purchasing.

How do I maintain my Rockbros cycling gear during summer?

Clean your Rockbros bags after muddy rides with mild soap and water, allow them to air dry completely, and store in a cool place. Avoid prolonged direct sunlight when not in use to preserve material integrity.

What's the difference between Rockbros handlebar bags and frame bags?

Handlebar bags mount on handlebars for quick access, ideal for phones and snacks. Frame bags attach to the bike frame for weight distribution and suit longer rides with more gear. Rockbros offers both options for different needs.
