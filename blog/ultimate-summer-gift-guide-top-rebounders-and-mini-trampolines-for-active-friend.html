Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a rebounder and a mini trampoline?

Rebounders are typically smaller, lower-profile bounce fitness tools designed specifically for cardio and fitness training, while mini trampolines are slightly larger and often used for recreational bouncing. Both offer excellent cardio benefits. Boogie Bounce makes high-quality versions of both types optimized for serious fitness work.

Are rebounders and mini trampolines safe for beginners?

Yes, when used correctly. Low-impact bouncing is actually easier on joints than running. Boogie Bounce equipment is designed with safety features like stable frames and quality springs. Start with gentle bouncing and progress gradually, and consult with a healthcare provider if you have specific concerns.
