  • Superior Cushioning (at least 6mm thickness): Joint pain demands extra padding. Standard mats at 3-4mm won't cut it.   • Textured, Non-Slip Surface: When you're managing pain, stability matters more than flexibility. A textured surface keeps your hands and feet anchored during poses, reducing compensatory movements that strain your joints.   • Joint-Friendly Materials (closed-cell or natural rubber): Avoid materials that break down quickly or off-gas chemicals that might irritate your body during extended sessions.
