Frequently Asked Questions

What brightness level is best for summer night riding?

For nighttime summer riding, Rockbros lights with 400-500+ lumens are ideal for illuminating the road ahead. For urban commuting with street lighting, 200-300 lumens is typically sufficient. Choose based on your riding environment and local lighting conditions.

Are Rockbros bike lights waterproof for summer rain?

Yes, most Rockbros bike lights feature waterproof or water-resistant construction, making them suitable for summer rainstorms and humid conditions. Check the IP rating on your specific model for detailed weather protection specifications.

How long does a Rockbros bike light battery last on a single charge?

Battery life varies by model and brightness setting. Most Rockbros rechargeable lights last 4-12 hours on a full charge. Lower brightness modes extend battery life, while maximum brightness reduces run time. Check your model's specifications for exact details.

Can Rockbros bike lights mount on handlebar bags or bike frames?

Yes, Rockbros lights come with versatile mounting options compatible with handlebars, bike bags, and frame attachment points. Many of their cycling accessories are designed to work together seamlessly for optimized visibility.

What's the difference between front and rear Rockbros bike lights?

Front lights provide powerful forward illumination for seeing the road, while rear lights focus on alerting traffic behind you. Rockbros offers specialized designs for each purpose—headlights with focused beams and tail lights with distinctive flash patterns for maximum safety.
