Frequently Asked Questions

Do these work with all power rack brands or only Rogue?

Monster Lite is Rogue's proprietary spacing standard (11-gauge holes at 2-inch intervals). They work with any compatible Monster Lite rack, which includes Rogue units and some third-party manufacturers who use the same spec. However, non-compatible brands (Titan, REP, some older units) require adapters or won't work at all. Verify your specific rack model before purchasing.

How much weight can these safely handle?

Rogue typically rates Monster Lite jammer arms for 500+ pounds of loaded weight on each arm, which is more than sufficient for most home gym users. Real-world durability holds up consistently per the 500+ verified reviews. The limiting factor is usually your own pressing strength, not equipment failure.

Do I need special attachments or handles to use these?

Base jammer arms accept standard Olympic plates or Rogue attachments. Some users prefer purchasing Rogue's dedicated jammer handles for ergonomics, which is an additional cost. Stock configuration works fine, but the optional handles do improve wrist position and comfort if you're doing high-rep work.

Can these replace a bench press setup?

Not entirely. Jammer arms provide fixed pressing planes, while bench pressing offers more shoulder mobility and stabilizer muscle recruitment. They're excellent for heavy compound work and injury prevention, but complementary to—not a replacement for—traditional pressing variations. Think of them as specialization equipment, not foundational.
