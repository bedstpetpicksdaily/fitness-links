How far can I ride on a single battery charge during summer?

Most Mooncool e-trikes offer 30-50 mile ranges per charge, depending on the model, terrain, and assist level. Summer temperatures typically don't significantly reduce range if the battery is properly maintained.

Mooncool e-trikes are designed to handle hot weather well. However, battery performance may decrease in temperatures above 95°F. Store your trike in shade when not in use and avoid leaving it in direct sunlight for extended periods.
