Frequently Asked Questions

How does the SF-B901 compare to traditional stationary bikes for home cardio?

The elliptical offers lower impact on joints compared to cycling, engaging more muscle groups (including glutes and hamstrings) through its striding motion. I found the SF-B901 less monotonous for extended sessions since the full-body engagement keeps things engaging. However, bikes take up less space. Choose based on your joint health and available space rather than assumed superiority of either machine.

Is the air resistance system better than magnetic or friction resistance?

Air resistance feels smoother and more natural because it adjusts instantly to your effort—push harder, it gets harder immediately. Magnetic systems can feel staged or artificial between resistance levels. The tradeoff is that air resistance is louder, which matters if you have a partner sleeping nearby. The SF-B901 produces a consistent whooshing sound that's audible but not deafening.

Can I use the SF-B901 for HIIT workouts or just steady-state cardio?

Absolutely use it for intervals. The responsive air resistance makes it excellent for HIIT because you can instantly increase intensity with harder leg drive. I've done 30-second sprint intervals followed by 30-second recovery periods without any lag in the machine's response. The frame stays stable throughout, which matters when you're generating maximum power output.
