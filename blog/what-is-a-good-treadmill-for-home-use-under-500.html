Key Features to Look For

When shopping for a treadmill under $500, prioritize motor power, running surface size, and incline range. The running deck should be at least 50 inches long and 18 inches wide to accommodate different stride lengths comfortably.

Motor and Performance

Budget-friendly treadmills typically feature continuous-duty motors rather than peak horsepower ratings. A continuous 2.0 HP motor will reliably support users up to 250-300 pounds and maintain consistent speed during workouts. Look for models with at least 10 speed levels (up to 10 mph minimum) and 5-10% incline capability to keep workouts varied and engaging.
