How quickly do Burchda folding electric bikes fold and unfold?

Can I fly with a Burchda folding electric bike?

Yes, with airline approval. Most folding e-bikes fit within luggage dimensions when collapsed. Always check your airline's specific requirements beforehand, and ensure the battery is properly disconnected or installed according to TSA guidelines.

Burchda batteries typically maintain 80% capacity after 800-1000 charge cycles (3-5 years of regular use). Summer heat can slightly reduce lifespan, so store your e-bike in cool conditions when not traveling.
