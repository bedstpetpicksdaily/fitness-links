Frequently Asked Questions

What weight dumbbells should a beginner start with?

Most beginners should start with 5-10 lbs dumbbells for upper body exercises and 8-15 lbs for lower body movements. Choose a weight where you can complete 12-15 reps with good form while feeling fatigued on the last few reps. You can always progress to heavier weights as you build strength over 2-4 weeks.

Are cheap dumbbells worth buying?

Budget dumbbells from brands like Yes4All, CAP Barbell, and Fitness Gear are absolutely worth buying for beginners and offer 80-90% of the performance of premium brands at half the price. The main trade-offs are durability and aesthetics rather than functionality, so they're perfect for starting your fitness journey without breaking the bank.

Should beginners buy adjustable or fixed weight dumbbells?

Adjustable dumbbells are better for beginners on a budget since one set replaces 5-10 individual dumbbells, saving money and space. However, if you only plan to do 2-3 basic exercises, fixed dumbbells are simpler and faster to use since you don't waste time adjusting weights between sets.

How much should I spend on beginner dumbbells?

A good beginner set of 2-3 pairs of fixed dumbbells costs $30-60 total, while adjustable dumbbells range from $50-150 depending on the weight range. Avoid the cheapest options under $20 as they often have poor grip and durability, but you don't need to spend more than $200 to get quality dumbbells that'll last years.
