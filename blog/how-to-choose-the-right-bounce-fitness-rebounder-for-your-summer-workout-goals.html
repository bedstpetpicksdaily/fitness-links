Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ideal rebounder size for a beginner?

Beginners typically benefit from 38-40 inch rebounders, which provide stability while taking up minimal space. Boogie Bounce offers excellent beginner models that balance safety with effective bounce quality.

How often should I use my bounce fitness rebounder?

For best results, aim for 20-30 minute sessions 3-4 times per week. Your Boogie Bounce rebounder is durable enough for frequent use, but listen to your body and adjust frequency based on your fitness level.

Is bounce fitness suitable for people with joint problems?

Yes, bounce fitness is exceptionally low-impact and often recommended for those with joint issues. The rebounder's surface absorbs impact, reducing stress on knees and ankles. Always consult with a healthcare provider before starting any new exercise program.

What's the difference between spring and bungee rebounders?

Spring rebounders offer more bounce and are better for intense cardio workouts, while bungee rebounders provide quieter, smoother bounces ideal for apartments. Boogie Bounce offers both types to suit different preferences.

How long do quality rebounders last?

Premium rebounders like those from Boogie Bounce can last 5-10+ years with proper care and maintenance, making them a worthwhile investment for your long-term fitness goals.
