Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Rockbros cycling gear ideal for hot weather riding?

Rockbros designs all their cycling accessories with heat management as a priority. Their helmets feature enhanced ventilation channels, gloves use breathable materials that wick moisture, and their compact bike lights reduce heat absorption. The lightweight construction across the entire product line minimizes additional body heat during intense summer rides.

Are Rockbros bike lights waterproof for summer rain?

Yes, Rockbros bike lights feature water-resistant and weatherproof construction rated for various weather conditions. They maintain full functionality during summer rain showers and maintain visibility in wet conditions, making them reliable year-round accessories.

Do Rockbros gloves work with smartphone touchscreens?

Most Rockbros cycling gloves designed for summer include touchscreen-compatible fingertips, allowing you to interact with your cycling computer, GPS, or smartphone without removing your gloves—a convenient feature for modern cyclists.

What capacity do Rockbros handlebar and bike bags offer?

Rockbros handlebar bags typically range from 1-3 liters with multiple organizational compartments, while their larger bike bags can hold 5-15 liters depending on the model. Most feature weatherproof materials and reflective accents for visibility and protection.

How do I choose the right Rockbros helmet size?

Rockbros helmets typically run small, so it's recommended to measure your head circumference carefully and consult their sizing chart. A proper fit should feel snug but not tight, with the helmet sitting level on your head approximately one inch above your eyebrows.
