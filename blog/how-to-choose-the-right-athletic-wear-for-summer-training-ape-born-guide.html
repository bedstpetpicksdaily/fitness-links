Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best fabric for summer athletic wear?

Look for moisture-wicking synthetic blends like polyester, nylon, or specialized performance fabrics. These materials pull sweat away from your skin and allow it to evaporate quickly. Avoid cotton, which absorbs moisture and becomes heavy when wet. APE BORN uses premium fabric blends specifically engineered for hot-weather training.

Should I wear light or dark colors for summer workouts?

Light colors reflect more sunlight and heat, keeping your body temperature lower during summer training. However, many people prefer darker colors for aesthetic reasons. APE BORN offers both options with UPF protection, so you can choose based on your preference while staying protected from UV rays.

How do I know if athletic wear has good ventilation?

Look for mesh panels, strategic cutouts, and perforated sections in areas prone to heat buildup like underarms and the back. APE BORN's premium gym gear features thoughtfully placed ventilation zones that maximize airflow without compromising durability or fit.
