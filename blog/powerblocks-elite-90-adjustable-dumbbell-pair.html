Frequently Asked Questions

How do PowerBlocks Elite 90s actually compare to Bowflex SelectTech at this weight?

Both hit similar final weights, but PowerBlocks prioritize compactness and feel closer to traditional dumbbells during loaded movements—they don't shift or flex under heavy pressing. SelectTech is cheaper and faster to adjust between weights. If you're doing heavy compound movements (bench, rows, overhead presses), PowerBlocks deliver more stability. If you're chasing time under tension or circuit training, SelectTech's faster mechanism matters more.

Will 90 lbs be heavy enough long-term for pressing movements?

For most home lifters, 90 lbs per dumbbell is the ceiling for unilateral pressing—going heavier requires either specialized equipment (incline benches with safety pins) or accepting form compromises. For pulling movements and carries, 90 lbs is genuinely heavy and provides room for progression over years. If you're already pressing 100+ lb dumbbells regularly, buy these knowing you'll hit the weight limit faster.

Is the storage stand necessary or just an upsell?

The stand isn't essential if you have rack space or floor room, but it becomes worth the money if your home gym is tight. PowerBlocks stack vertically either way—the stand just makes them more accessible and safer around kids or pets. Buy the stand only if you lack dedicated rack space.
