Why E-Trikes Are Perfect for Hot Weather Riding

Electric tricycles offer distinct advantages over traditional bikes when temperatures soar. Additionally, the lower center of gravity of e-trikes makes them ideal for seniors and those seeking a safer, more comfortable riding experience.
