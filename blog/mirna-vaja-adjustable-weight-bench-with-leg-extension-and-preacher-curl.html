Frequently Asked Questions

Is this bench stable enough for heavy dumbbell work?

Yes, for dumbbells up to about 80-100 pounds per side. The frame won't wobble, and the backrest holds firm. If you're regularly lifting heavier, you need a dedicated commercial bench. For the average home lifter doing 30-60 pound dumbbells, this is solid.

How much space does it actually take up when folded or stored?

It doesn't fully fold into a compact package like some benches claim. The leg extension attachment is fixed, so you're looking at roughly 6 feet long by 3 feet wide even in its most compact position. Measure your space before ordering—this isn't a tuck-under-the-bed solution.

Is the leg extension weight stack adjustable or resistance-based?

It's resistance-based using a pin system, not a weight stack. You adjust difficulty by changing the pin position, similar to cable machines. No grinding gears or plate loading needed, which means faster adjustments between exercises but less precision for tracking progress.
