Is this better than a treadmill for home workouts?

Different goals, different answers. Treadmills are versatile (walking, jogging, sprinting) and easier to use casually. Stair steppers isolate lower body and glutes harder but demand intensity and form. Treadmills also have lower maintenance and quieter operation. If you want cardio flexibility, treadmill wins.
