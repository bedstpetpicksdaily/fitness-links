What's the best Rockbros product for a beginner cyclist?

Start with essential safety items: a reliable Rockbros helmet and bike lights. Add a comfortable pair of cycling gloves and a handlebar bag for phone access. These foundational pieces provide safety, comfort, and convenience without overwhelming new riders.

Are Rockbros bike lights waterproof for summer storms?

Yes, Rockbros bike lights feature weather-resistant ratings suitable for summer weather. Most models include water-resistant coatings and sealed components, though checking specific product specs ensures you choose the right weatherproofing level for your riding conditions.

How do I choose the right Rockbros helmet size?

Measure your head circumference just above your eyebrows with a flexible tape measure. Rockbros provides detailed sizing charts with each helmet. A proper fit should feel snug without pressure points, with the helmet sitting level on your head about an inch above your eyebrows.

What makes Rockbros handlebar bags different from other brands?

Rockbros handlebar bags combine durable, weather-resistant materials with thoughtful compartment designs. They offer easy access to essentials without adding excessive weight, and their ergonomic attachment systems work seamlessly with various handlebar styles.

Do Rockbros cycling gloves work with touchscreen phones?

Many Rockbros glove models feature touchscreen-compatible fingertips, allowing you to use your phone without removing gloves. Check product descriptions to confirm this feature on your chosen style.
