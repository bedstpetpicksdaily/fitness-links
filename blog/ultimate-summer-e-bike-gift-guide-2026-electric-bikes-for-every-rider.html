Frequently Asked Questions

How far can Burchda e-bikes travel on a single charge?

Most Burchda electric bikes offer 40-80+ miles of range depending on the model, battery capacity, terrain, and riding mode. Factors like rider weight, pedal-assist level, and weather conditions affect actual range.

Are electric bikes suitable for beginners?

Absolutely! E-bikes are ideal for beginners as pedal-assist technology makes riding easier and less intimidating. Burchda's user-friendly designs and responsive motors help new riders build confidence quickly.

How long do e-bike batteries last?

Quality e-bike batteries like those in Burchda models typically last 3-5 years or 500-1000 charge cycles. Proper maintenance and charging habits can extend battery lifespan significantly.

Can I ride an e-bike in the rain?

Yes, Burchda e-bikes feature weather-resistant components and sealed connectors. However, avoid deep water crossings and allow time to dry after heavy rain for optimal performance.

What's the difference between hub and mid-drive motors?

Hub motors are integrated into the wheel and offer lower maintenance, while mid-drive motors provide better weight distribution and hill-climbing ability. Burchda offers both options depending on your riding needs.
