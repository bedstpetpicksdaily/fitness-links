Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories does an under desk treadmill burn?

An under desk treadmill typically burns 100-200 calories per hour at a moderate walking pace of 2-4 mph, depending on your body weight and intensity. This is significantly lower than traditional treadmills because you're walking slowly while working, but it adds meaningful daily movement and can contribute 500-1000+ calories burned per week with consistent use.

Can you actually work while using an under desk treadmill?

Yes, most people can work effectively while walking at slower speeds (2-3 mph), especially for tasks like emails, calls, and computer work that don't require intense focus. However, typing and detailed work become difficult at faster speeds above 3.5 mph, so it's best suited for lighter office tasks.

What's the weight capacity for compact under desk treadmills?

Most compact under desk treadmills have weight capacities between 220-265 lbs, though some premium models support up to 300 lbs. Check the specific model's specifications before purchasing, as this directly impacts durability and safety during use.

How much space do you need for an under desk treadmill?

Compact under desk treadmills typically measure 47-55 inches long, 30 inches wide, and 4-5 inches tall, fitting underneath most standard desks without modification. Before buying, measure your desk space and ensure there's at least 2 feet of clearance behind your desk for safe operation and to avoid kicking the back during use.
