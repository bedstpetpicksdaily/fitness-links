Frequently Asked Questions

Will this dolly handle a treadmill?

Most residential treadmills weigh 200-300 pounds—well within the 600-pound capacity. The key is securing it properly with the built-in straps and ensuring you have clear pathways. Heavier commercial treadmills (500+ pounds) are riskier; check your specific model's weight first.

Do pneumatic wheels really matter, or are solid wheels fine?

Pneumatic wheels reduce strain on your joints and equipment during movement, plus they're quieter on hard floors. Solid wheels are maintenance-free but feel clunky and can skip across transitions. For home gym use, pneumatic is worth it—just commit to checking pressure monthly.

Can you fit this through standard doorways?

Standard US doorways are 32-36 inches wide. At 16 inches, the dolly fits through, but barely if you're at an angle with large equipment stacked on top. Measure your narrowest doorway and add a few inches of safety margin before ordering.
