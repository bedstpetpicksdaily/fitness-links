Frequently Asked Questions

How bright should a bike light be for night riding?

For urban commuting, 400-600 lumens is typically sufficient. Off-road and trail riding benefits from 800+ lumens for better visibility of terrain ahead. Rockbros offers models across this range to match your specific riding environment.

Are Rockbros bike lights waterproof?

Yes, Rockbros bike lights feature IP65 waterproof ratings, meaning they're protected against water jets from any direction and safe to use in heavy rain, snow, and wet conditions without performance degradation.

How long does the battery last on Rockbros bike lights?

Battery life varies by model and brightness mode. Front lights typically last 8-15 hours on full brightness, while steady or lower modes can extend to 20+ hours. Rear lights often offer 15-30+ hours depending on the flashing pattern selected.

Can I use Rockbros bike lights on any bicycle?

Rockbros bike lights feature universal mounting brackets that fit most handlebar diameters and seat post sizes. However, it's recommended to check compatibility with your specific bike model before purchase.

Do Rockbros lights come with mounting hardware?
