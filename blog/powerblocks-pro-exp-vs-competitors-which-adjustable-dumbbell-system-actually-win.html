Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need all three PowerBlocks stages, or can I start with just Stage 1?

You can absolutely start with Stage 1—it's designed as an entry point for beginners building foundational strength. However, if you know you'll reach heavier weights, buying Stage 1 now and stages 2-3 later costs more total than purchasing the full 5-90 lb set upfront. Start with Stage 1 only if your budget legitimately doesn't allow for the full set, or if you're genuinely uncertain whether home training will stick long-term.

Will PowerBlocks Pro EXP work for bench press and other heavy compound lifts?

Yes. The 90 lb per-dumbbell ceiling is heavy enough for dumbbell bench press, rows, shoulder press, and most home gym compound movements. The only exception: advanced lifters regularly working with heavier dumbbells for lower rep ranges. For most home gyms, 90 lbs per hand is sufficient for years of progression.

Is the 5-90 lb range actually enough, or will I outgrow it?

For 80% of home gym users, no—you won't outgrow 90 lbs per dumbbell. Linear progression programs stop requiring heavier dumbbells long before you hit that ceiling. Advanced lifters doing high-rep, specialized isolation work might eventually need heavier options, but that's a minority of users. The real question is whether you'll stick with strength training long enough to max out, not whether the range is theoretically sufficient.

How do PowerBlocks Pro EXP compare to cheaper adjustable dumbbells on price?

Cheaper models cost 40-60% less upfront, but real-world data shows they fail within 3-12 months through grip degradation, adjustment mechanism failure, or handle cracking. No. The textured grip has been verified to hold up reliably through eight months of regular use without smoothing out or becoming slippery. This matters for exercises like farmer's carries or when moving quickly between movements with sweat on your hands.
