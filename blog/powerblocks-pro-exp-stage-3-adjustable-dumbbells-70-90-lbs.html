If you're intermediate or advanced, you'll outgrow it within 2-3 months of consistent training. Most users buy stages progressively as they actually need the weight, rather than all at once. How do these compare to Bowflex SelectTech for someone with limited space?

PowerBlocks take up less vertical space and adjust faster (about 3 seconds vs. 8-10 for dial systems). However, Bowflex's dial adjustment feels slightly smoother if you're doing rapid drop sets. PowerBlocks win on compactness; Bowflex wins on feel during weight selection. For a small bedroom or apartment, PowerBlocks edge ahead because they literally take up less room on the shelf.

Will the adjustment mechanism fail after heavy use, or is it truly durable?

That said, handle the adjustment pin carefully (don't force it), and they'll last years. I'd estimate 5+ years of regular use before any wear becomes noticeable, based on how my current pair is holding up and user reports online.
