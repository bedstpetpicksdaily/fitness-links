Frequently Asked Questions

How do these compare to PowerBlocks or Bowflex SelectTech?

Yes4All uses a simpler dial system that's faster to adjust than some competitors but less tech-forward than Bowflex's electronic options. PowerBlocks are lighter overall but typically cost 30-50% more. For the money, Yes4All wins on value. For absolute premium construction and warranty backing, PowerBlocks edge ahead. It depends whether you're optimizing for budget or for luxury durability.

Will the adjustment mechanism fail after a year?

The 500+ verified reviews suggest durability is solid, with most complaints being about initial stiffness (which loosens with use), not breakdown. You'll see some users reporting issues around 18-24 months of heavy daily use, but that's the exception, not the rule. Treat them normally and they'll last years. Use them like you're testing a tank, and you might encounter problems sooner.

Are these actually quieter than dropping fixed dumbbells?

Yes, notably. The rubber baseplate and compact design mean less clang and fewer vibration complaints from downstairs neighbors. They're not silent—you're still dropping weight on a hard floor—but they're measurably quieter than traditional dumbbells. If noise is your main concern, consider rubber hex dumbbells instead. If it's secondary to convenience, these win.
