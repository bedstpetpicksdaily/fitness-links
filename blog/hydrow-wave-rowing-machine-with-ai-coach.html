Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Coach compare to Peloton's digital coaching?

The Hydrow Wave's AI is more technically sophisticated for rowing-specific form correction—it actually uses pose detection rather than just audio cues. Peloton's strength is class variety and community engagement. For pure rowing coaching, Hydrow's system is superior, but Peloton wins on variety and social features. Most serious rowers prefer Hydrow's narrow focus.

Can you use this with other apps or is it locked to Hydrow's ecosystem?

The machine connects to Hydrow's proprietary app primarily, which is both a strength and limitation. You get curated workouts specifically designed for this hardware, but you can't freely import workouts from other platforms like Concept2 or ErgData. This matters less if you're satisfied with the 50+ built-in programs, but it does lock you into their ecosystem.

What's the noise level during intense efforts?

Magnetic rowers are inherently quieter than water or air models, and the Hydrow Wave is no exception—around 65-70dB during maximum effort, roughly equivalent to a dishwasher running. You won't disturb neighbors on shared walls, though roommates will definitely know you're working. It's the quietest option in this interactive category.

Is this suitable for complete beginners or experienced rowers?

Both, genuinely. The AI adjusts difficulty scaling based on your fitness level during onboarding, so beginners won't feel overwhelmed, but the machine has enough resistance and programming depth to challenge someone with years of rowing experience. I've seen both demographics represented in that 122-review pool with similar satisfaction levels.

How often does the software update and add new workouts?

Hydrow typically releases new workouts monthly and performance improvements quarterly. I haven't experienced major bugs, and the system stays current. However, unlike some competitors, they don't do radical interface overhauls—it's conservative updates focused on stability over flashiness, which honestly works in their favor for a fitness tool where reliability matters.
