Understanding Traditional Bikes

Traditional bicycles have been the gold standard for outdoor recreation for over a century. They offer simplicity, affordability, and a genuine sense of physical accomplishment. Riders get a full-body workout, improved cardiovascular health, and the satisfaction of propelling themselves forward through pure human effort. Traditional bikes are lightweight, require minimal maintenance compared to motorized vehicles, and come in countless styles for different terrains and purposes.

However, traditional bikes demand significant physical exertion, especially for seniors or those with mobility limitations. Uphill climbs, long distances, and adverse weather conditions can quickly become exhausting. Additionally, balance and coordination are essential, which may pose challenges for older adults or those recovering from injuries.
