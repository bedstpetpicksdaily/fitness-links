The water tank is your machine's heart. Polycarbonate tanks are lighter and more affordable, but ash wood tanks provide superior aesthetics and longevity. If space is tight, you want a machine that lasts—invest in quality materials that won't degrade or leak after 2-3 years of regular use.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much space do you need for a water rower machine?

Most water rower machines are between 7-8 feet long and 2-3 feet wide, making them suitable for apartments and home gyms with limited space. When not in use, many models can be stored vertically or folded, reducing their footprint to just 3-4 feet long. Before purchasing, measure your space and ensure you have at least 8 feet of clearance lengthwise for comfortable rowing strokes.

What's the best compact water rower for small apartments?

Compact water rowers like the Sunny Health & Fitness Obsidian and WaterRower Natural are designed for smaller spaces at 7-8 feet long with foldable or vertical storage options. These machines maintain the smooth, low-impact rowing experience while taking up minimal floor space compared to standard models. Look for models with footprints under 20 square feet if space is your primary concern.

Does a water rower take up less space than a treadmill?

Water rowers are generally more compact than treadmills—they're typically 7-8 feet long versus a treadmill's 6-7 feet wide, but rowers can be stored more easily due to their vertical or foldable designs. Many water rowers can be stored upright against a wall or in a corner, while treadmills require dedicated floor space. If storage flexibility is important, a water rower is usually the better choice for small spaces.

Can you put a water rower in a small bedroom or closet?

While some ultra-compact water rowers can fit in larger bedrooms when stored vertically, most require a dedicated space like a living room corner or home gym area due to their length and the noise during use. Storing one in a closet isn't practical since you need clearance to use it, and the water tank requires climate control to prevent mold. A corner of a bedroom with at least 8 feet of open space is feasible if it's your only option.
