Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between fat tire and regular e-bikes?

Fat tire e-bikes feature wider, knobby tires (4-5 inches) that provide superior traction, comfort, and stability on sand, snow, and rough terrain. Regular tires are narrower and better for paved surfaces. Burchda offers both options depending on your primary riding environment.

How far can I ride on a single charge?

Range depends on battery capacity, motor power, terrain, and rider weight. Most Burchda e-bikes offer 25-60 miles per charge. Fat tire and commuter models typically feature larger batteries for extended range on summer adventures.

Are folding e-bikes as durable as full-size models?

Yes, quality folding e-bikes like those from Burchda are built with durable materials and robust hinges. They perform equally well as full-size models while offering convenient portability for storage and multi-modal commuting.

What maintenance do Burchda e-bikes require?

Regular e-bike maintenance includes keeping tires properly inflated, checking brakes, lubricating the chain, and maintaining battery health. Burchda e-bikes are engineered for reliability with minimal maintenance needs compared to traditional bikes.

Can I use an e-bike in rain and summer weather?

Yes, Burchda e-bikes feature weather-resistant components designed to withstand rain and summer conditions. However, avoid submerging the battery and motor, and perform regular maintenance after wet rides.
