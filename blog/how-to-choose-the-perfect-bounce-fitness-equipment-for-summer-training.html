Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a rebounder and a mini trampoline?

Rebounders typically feature springs and are designed for fitness training with various bounce heights and resistance levels. Mini trampolines often have springs or elastic cords and are generally smaller. Boogie Bounce offers both options, clearly labeling each for specific training needs.

Is bounce fitness equipment safe for beginners?

Yes, bounce fitness is extremely safe for beginners when you use quality equipment like Boogie Bounce. Start with gentle bouncing, focus on form, and gradually increase intensity. The low-impact nature makes it ideal for newcomers and those with joint concerns.

How much space do I need for bounce fitness equipment?

Boogie Bounce mini trampolines require minimal space—often just 3-4 feet in diameter. Even their larger rebounders fit comfortably in most living rooms, bedrooms, or home gyms. Measure your space before purchasing to ensure proper fit.

Can I use bounce fitness equipment outdoors during summer?

Absolutely! Boogie Bounce equipment is designed for durability. Place your rebounder on a flat surface under a patio or shade structure. Just ensure you protect it from extreme weather when not in use to maintain longevity.

What's the weight capacity of Boogie Bounce equipment?

Most Boogie Bounce rebounders and mini trampolines support 250-300+ pounds, depending on the specific model. Check the product specifications on their website to find the right fit for your weight and fitness needs.
