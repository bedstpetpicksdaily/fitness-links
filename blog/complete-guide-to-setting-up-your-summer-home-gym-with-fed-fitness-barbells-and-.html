Frequently Asked Questions

What size barbell should I choose for my home gym?

Olympic barbells (45 lbs/20 kg) are standard for most home gyms, but FED Fitness also offers lighter options for beginners. Consider your strength level, available space, and training goals when selecting barbell weight and style.

Do I need both bumper plates and iron plates from FED Fitness?

It depends on your training style and floor type. Bumper plates are ideal for explosive movements and protect flooring, while iron plates are more economical for general strength training. Most home gym enthusiasts start with iron plates and add bumpers as their gym expands.

Can FED Fitness equipment withstand outdoor summer weather?

Yes, FED Fitness equipment is manufactured with durability in mind. Iron plates and barbells are weather-resistant, though covering them during extreme weather and storing them properly extends their lifespan significantly.

What's the minimum investment to start a FED Fitness home gym?

A basic starter setup with one barbell, weight plates, and an adjustable bench typically ranges from $300-$600, depending on your specifications. You can expand from there based on your budget and training needs.

Should I invest in a power rack for my summer home gym?

A power rack is highly recommended if you plan to perform heavy barbell squats, bench presses, or other compound lifts. It provides essential safety features and enables progressive training. FED Fitness offers affordable power rack options suitable for home use.
