Start with a Quality Barbell and Weight Plates

The foundation of any serious home gym is a quality barbell. FED Fitness offers Olympic barbells that meet professional standards while remaining accessible for home use. When selecting a barbell, consider the shaft diameter (typically 28mm for women, 29mm for men), weight capacity, and knurling pattern for grip security. FED Fitness barbells feature aggressive knurling and reliable load-bearing capacity, making them suitable for both beginners learning proper form and advanced lifters pushing heavy weights.

Pairing your barbell with quality weight plates is equally important. FED Fitness provides durable plates in various weights, allowing you to progress gradually and scale your workouts. Olympic plates are standardized, meaning any FED Fitness plate will work with any Olympic barbell, giving you flexibility to expand your collection over time.

Choose the Right Weight Bench for Stability and Comfort

A quality weight bench is invaluable for exercises like bench press, dumbbell rows, and seated shoulder work. FED Fitness weight benches combine stability with comfort, featuring adjustable backrest positions to target different muscle groups. Whether you need a flat bench for pure strength work or an adjustable bench for varied exercises, FED Fitness options accommodate different training styles and space constraints. Look for benches with sturdy leg supports and non-slip surfaces to ensure safety during heavy lifts.

Add Dumbbells and Accessory Equipment

FED Fitness dumbbells complement barbells perfectly for isolation exercises and accessory work. Dumbbell training allows for greater range of motion and helps address muscle imbalances. Consider starting with adjustable dumbbells to save space, or invest in fixed dumbbells if you prefer rapid weight transitions. Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum equipment needed to start a home gym?

At minimum, you need a barbell, weight plates, and an adjustable bench from FED Fitness. These three essentials allow hundreds of exercises targeting all major muscle groups. Add dumbbells as your budget allows for greater exercise variety.

How much space do I need for a home gym with FED Fitness equipment?

A basic setup requires about 100-150 square feet. A barbell, bench, and dumbbell rack fit comfortably in this space. Power racks and extensive equipment collections benefit from 200+ square feet, but FED Fitness offers compact options for smaller spaces.

Are FED Fitness barbells compatible with standard Olympic plates?

Yes, FED Fitness Olympic barbells are standardized and compatible with all Olympic plates, including FED Fitness plates and plates from other brands. This compatibility provides flexibility when expanding your equipment collection.

What weight bench features matter most for home training?

Look for stability, adjustability, and comfort. FED Fitness weight benches feature sturdy construction, multiple backrest angles, and secure leg supports. Adjustable benches offer more exercise variety, while flat benches excel for pure strength work.

Should I buy dumbbells or adjustable weights for my home gym?

Fixed dumbbells from FED Fitness allow faster weight changes and durability, while adjustable dumbbells save space and money initially. Choose based on your available space, budget, and training preference. Many home gyms benefit from both.
