Frequently Asked Questions

How far can Burchda electric bikes travel on a single charge?

Most Burchda commuter e-bikes offer 50-100+ miles of range depending on the model, motor power, battery capacity, rider weight, and terrain. Fat tire and folding models typically range 40-80 miles, making them ideal for daily summer commuting without daily charging requirements.

Are Burchda electric bikes waterproof for summer rain commutes?

Burchda e-bikes feature weather-resistant construction with sealed connectors and protected electronics. While not fully waterproof, they're designed to handle rain and moisture typical of summer weather. Avoid heavy submersion and dry components after wet rides to maximize longevity.

What's the difference between Burchda fat tire and folding electric bikes?

Burchda's fat tire e-bikes offer superior traction, stability, and comfort on rough terrain with wider tires and suspension systems. Folding e-bikes prioritize portability and storage, featuring compact designs that collapse for easy transport on public transit or apartment storage—perfect for multi-modal urban commutes.

How long does a Burchda e-bike battery last before needing replacement?

Burchda batteries typically last 3-5 years with proper care and maintenance. Lifespan depends on charging frequency, storage conditions, and usage patterns. Most models retain 80% capacity after 500-1000 charge cycles, ensuring reliable performance throughout multiple summer seasons.

Can I use a Burchda electric bike for off-road summer adventures?

Yes—Burchda's fat tire electric bikes are specifically engineered for off-road capability. With sturdy frames, wide knobby tires, and powerful motors, they handle trails, sand, snow, and rocky terrain. However, their standard commuter models are optimized for paved surfaces and light gravel paths.
