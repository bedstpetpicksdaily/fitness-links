Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a mini trampoline and a rebounder?

Mini trampolines and rebounders are essentially the same thing—small, stationary trampolines designed for fitness. The term 'rebounder' emphasizes the fitness and bounce benefits, while 'mini trampoline' is more general. Quality rebounders like Boogie Bounce models are engineered specifically for bounce fitness rather than recreational jumping.

Are mini trampolines safe for children?

Yes, quality mini trampolines with proper safety features are safe for children. Look for models with padded edges, sturdy construction, and appropriate weight limits. Boogie Bounce rebounders include safety padding and are designed to handle multiple users safely. Always supervise young children and follow manufacturer guidelines.

How much bounce fitness exercise do I need for health benefits?

Research suggests 15-30 minutes of rebounding 3-5 times weekly provides cardiovascular benefits. Start with 10-15 minute sessions and gradually increase duration. The beauty of Boogie Bounce mini trampolines is they make exercise so fun that families naturally exceed recommended activity levels.

What weight capacity should I look for in a rebounder?

Choose a mini trampoline with a weight capacity at least 100+ pounds above your heaviest user. This ensures stability and durability. Boogie Bounce rebounders offer various capacity options to accommodate different family sizes and fitness levels.

Do mini trampolines require much maintenance?

Quality rebounders require minimal maintenance. Occasionally check springs and padding, keep the surface clean, and store indoors when not in use. Boogie Bounce bounce fitness equipment is designed for durability with low maintenance requirements.
