What to Look For

1. BTU Capacity (Right-Sized Cooling Power)

BTU (British Thermal Units) measures cooling capacity. For a garage gym, you'll need:

  • 10,000-12,000 BTU for small gyms (under 200 sq ft)
  • 14,000-15,000 BTU for medium gyms (200-300 sq ft)
  • 18,000+ BTU for large or very hot garages (300+ sq ft)

Undersized units won't keep up; oversized units waste energy. Measure your garage first.

2. Noise Level (Train Without Distraction)

Most portable ACs range from 50-75 decibels. For a workout environment, look for units rated under 65 decibels—that's comparable to normal conversation. Check reviews specifically mentioning noise during operation; this is where cheap units fail.

3. Exhaust Hose Installation (Ease and Flexibility)

Portable ACs require venting hot air outside through a window or wall. The best models feature:

  • Flexible, extendable exhaust hoses (up to 8 feet)
  • Easy-install window kits that don't require permanent modifications
  • Adjustable vent configurations for garage-specific setups

This is critical—poor venting means the unit can't cool effectively.

4. Energy Efficiency and Operating Costs

Look for units with:

  • EER (Energy Efficiency Ratio) of 8 or higher
  • Programmable thermostats and timers to avoid running 24/7
  • Auto-shutoff features when target temperature is reached

A highly efficient portable AC costs $0.50-$1.00 per hour to operate, versus $1.50+ for budget models.
