    Frequently Asked Questions

    Are latex free resistance bands as durable as latex bands?

    Yes, high-quality latex-free bands made from natural rubber or TPE (thermoplastic elastomer) are just as durable as latex bands, often lasting 1-2 years with regular use. The key is choosing reputable brands that use premium materials, as cheaper latex-free options may degrade faster or lose elasticity over time.

    Can I use latex free resistance bands for physical therapy if I have a latex allergy?

    Absolutely—latex-free resistance bands are specifically designed for people with latex allergies and are completely safe for therapeutic use. They provide the same resistance and support needed for rehabilitation exercises without triggering allergic reactions.

    What resistance level should I choose for post-injury rehabilitation?

    Start with light resistance (typically yellow or red bands) for early-stage rehabilitation and gradually progress to medium (green) or heavy (blue) as your strength and mobility improve. Your physical therapist can recommend the specific resistance level based on your injury and recovery stage.

    Do latex free resistance bands work as well as weights for building strength?

    Latex-free resistance bands are excellent for building strength and offer advantages over weights, including variable resistance that increases as you stretch the band. They're particularly effective for rehabilitation because they're low-impact, safer on recovering joints, and allow for greater range of motion.
