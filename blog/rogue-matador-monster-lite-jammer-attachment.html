Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need this if I already have a bench and squat rack?

Not necessarily. If you're doing standard bench press, squat, and deadlift work, you're covered. This shines for intermediate+ lifters who want horizontal pressing variations (high-to-low pressing angles, single-arm work) that a flat bench can't provide. It's an accessory for accessory work, not a replacement for fundamental equipment.

Will this work with my non-Rogue rack?

Only if your uprights match Monster Lite spacing (11mm holes, 2-inch spacing). Rogue racks do. If you have a different brand, check your manual—many budget racks use incompatible dimensions. Forcing incompatible attachments leads to stripped holes and wasted money.

How much weight can it safely handle?

Rogue rates this for serious loading without published max limits because it depends on your frame and bolt quality. Owners routinely report using it with 225+ lb loaded safely, and the 500+ reviews averaging 4.3 stars include zero reports of structural failure. That said, this isn't the place to test your one-rep max if you haven't bolted it down properly.
