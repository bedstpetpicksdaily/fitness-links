Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between an electric tricycle and an electric bike?

Electric tricycles offer three-wheel stability versus two wheels on e-bikes, making them ideal for seniors and those with balance concerns. E-trikes provide more comfortable seating, larger cargo capacity, and feel more secure at lower speeds.

How long does a Mooncool electric tricycle battery last?

Mooncool lithium batteries typically last 3-5 years with proper maintenance. Most models offer 30-50 miles per charge, depending on terrain, rider weight, and pedal-assist level used during rides.

Are Mooncool e-trikes suitable for seniors with limited mobility?

Yes. Mooncool designs specifically address senior needs with low step-through frames, adjustable seats, throttle-only modes requiring no pedaling, and stability features. Consult your doctor before starting any new physical activity.

What's the typical price range for Mooncool electric tricycles?

Mooncool e-trikes range from approximately $800 for basic models to $3,500+ for premium versions with extended batteries, enhanced motors, and advanced suspension systems.

Do I need a license or registration to ride an electric tricycle?

Regulations vary by location. Most U.S. states don't require licensing for e-trikes under 750W, but check your local laws regarding speed limits, helmet requirements, and where you can ride.
