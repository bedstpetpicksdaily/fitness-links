What to Look For

  • Compact Footprint When Folded: Look for treadmills that fold down to roughly 30" x 20" or smaller. Measure your actual storage space before buying—whether that's a closet, under your bed, or behind the couch. Some models fold vertically to save floor space, while others fold horizontally. Choose based on where you'll actually store it, not just where you think you might.
  • Lightweight and Easy-Fold Mechanism: A treadmill that weighs over 200 pounds becomes a storage nightmare. Aim for models under 180 pounds that have simple, one-handed folding mechanisms with safety locks. If you're the only one using it, you need to be able to fold and move it yourself without struggle or risk of pinching fingers.
